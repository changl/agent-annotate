**Cycles to done: how to count it objectively.**

- A cycle is the work coming back to you and you sending it on. Both ways are already stored: a Send round, or a message you type to an agent.
- It is counted per goal, from the day you state it to the day you accept it. So it does not matter that late cycles move a project less than early ones.
- Each cycle gets one cause, from what you clicked: your decision (plan, look and feel, go-live), sent back, should not have been asked, or your change. The first kind is the goal's par. The second and third are waste, and their target is zero.
- The brag reads "done in 4 cycles, par 3". Progress per cycle is the share of the goal's acceptance checks that pass after each cycle, drawn as one curve per goal.
- Today it cannot be counted per goal, because a question is not tied to a goal. For agent-annotate as a whole: 108 answers in 36 sittings, for 172 finished items.
