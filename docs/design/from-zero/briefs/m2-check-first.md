# M2. The machine, designed from the checks outward

Your full brief is `docs/design/from-zero/briefs/m-machine.md`. Read it first and follow it exactly.

Your file: `docs/design/from-zero/machine/check-first.md`.

Your starting centre: **the agreement is a contract that can be checked.** A goal is its "done" lines, and each "done" line is something that passes or fails. The machine exists to turn his sentence into checkable lines, to make them pass, and to prove it. Checks are written before the work. Progress is the count of lines passing. A showing is the proof laid out for him. Trust is the history of lines that passed first time and stayed passed. Doers are interchangeable: anything that can make a failing line pass may try.

The known way this centre fails: what matters most to him is often what cannot be checked by a machine (taste, feel, "is this what I meant"); checks get written to be passable rather than true; and work that passes every line is still not what he wanted. Your design must beat that or say honestly that it cannot.

You may leave the centre wherever the method demands it. Say where and why.
