# MC1. Judge the four machine designs: what breaks

Your full brief is `docs/design/from-zero/briefs/mc-machine-critic.md`. Read it first and follow it exactly.

Your file: `docs/design/from-zero/research/machine-critique-breaks.md`.

Your lens: **what breaks.** Try to break each design. For each of the failures below, say for each design what catches it, how soon, at what cost, and what he sees. Mark it `not caught` when nothing does.

1. A helper stops silently in the middle of work.
2. A helper waits on something that will never come.
3. A message between parts is lost, late, or read by the wrong part.
4. A helper says "done" and it is not.
5. A helper restarts and has forgotten.
6. Two helpers change the same thing.
7. A computer is off for a night.
8. The record cannot be reached.
9. The record is wrong: it says something passed that did not, or is hours out of date.
10. The watch itself has stopped.
11. The checker shares the maker's blind spot and passes bad work.
12. A default acts on something he never gave authority for.
13. The gate holds back something he needed to know.
14. A rule is applied where it does not belong, or two rules conflict.
15. Two goals need the same thing at once, or one undoes the other.
16. He answers nothing for five days.
17. Ten goals become forty.
18. A result he accepted turns out wrong after it has been built upon.
19. An action that cannot be undone (money, a public release, a message to real people) is about to be taken on a wrong assumption.
20. He stops believing "nothing needs you". What, in each design, would have caused that, and what would win his belief back?

End your lens section with: the one failure most likely to destroy his trust in each design, and the protections that every design must have regardless of which is chosen.
