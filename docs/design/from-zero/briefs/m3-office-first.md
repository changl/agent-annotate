# M3. The machine, designed as a well-run private office

Your full brief is `docs/design/from-zero/briefs/m-machine.md`. Read it first and follow it exactly.

Your file: `docs/design/from-zero/machine/office-first.md`.

Your starting centre: **the procedures of a chief of staff's office.** Such offices have served one busy principal for a century with paper and discipline: one in-tray and one out-tray; a briefing book prepared before each sitting; a bring-forward file that returns each matter on its day; standing orders that say what the staff may decide alone; a decision log; the rule that nothing goes to the principal unprepared, and that every paper says what is asked of him and what happens if he does not answer; read-back of every instruction; a duty officer who watches the clock. Start from those procedures, find the ones that carry over, and design the machine as that office with helpers in the staff roles and scripts as the clerks.

The known way this centre fails: offices grow layers; staff protect the principal from things he needed to know; procedure becomes the work; and what worked with five matters a day breaks with five hundred. Your design must beat that or say honestly that it cannot.

You may leave the centre wherever the method demands it. Say where and why.
