# C1. Critic: the strict count

Your full brief is `docs/design/from-zero/briefs/c-critic.md`. Read it first and follow it exactly.

Your name: `count`. Your file: `docs/design/from-zero/research/critique-count.md`.

Your emphasis: **one counting rule, applied the same way to all four.** The four designers counted their own weeks and did not count alike (one measured words with a script, others estimated; some added a goal to the scenario). Their totals cannot be compared as they stand. Drive each prototype yourself through all 14 events and count, with one frozen rule that you write down first:

- places he must look;
- interruptions (anything he did not choose to open);
- things he must hold in his head between sittings, including habits he must keep;
- words he must read to act, counted separately from words that are merely available;
- words typed and words spoken;
- taps or keys;
- decisions, how many only he could make, and how many were acceptances of something a check had already passed;
- minutes, with your reading-speed and thinking-time assumptions stated.

Give the comparable table, say where each designer's own count was wrong and by how much, and say which differences between concepts are real and which are only differences in how much text the designer chose to write.
