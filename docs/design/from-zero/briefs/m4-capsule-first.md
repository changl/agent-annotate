# M4. The machine, designed around goals that carry themselves

Your full brief is `docs/design/from-zero/briefs/m-machine.md`. Read it first and follow it exactly.

Your file: `docs/design/from-zero/machine/capsule-first.md`.

Your starting centre: **each goal is a capsule that carries everything about itself.** His words, the agreement, the "done" lines and their checks, the rulings that apply, the work so far, the proof, what happens next and when: all of it travels together as one self-contained thing. Any helper on any computer that is handed the capsule can advance it one step and hand it back, knowing nothing else. There is no central brain that must be up, no long-lived coordinator, and nothing to hand over because the capsule is the hand-over. What he sees is assembled from the capsules.

The known way this centre fails: things that cut across goals have no home (his preferences, the cap on what reaches him, two goals touching the same thing, the picture of everything at once); capsules drift apart from each other and from the truth; and someone still has to notice a capsule that has stopped moving. Your design must beat that or say honestly that it cannot.

You may leave the centre wherever the method demands it. Say where and why.
