# Capsule first: the goal carries the work

## 1. The idea

Each goal is a travelling capsule: his words, the agreement, authority, checks, rulings, work, proof and the next action travel together, so a fresh helper can advance it without a conversation. Short runs open it, do one bounded job and return a new version; scripts assemble his one doorway from the capsules. I leave the pure capsule premise at shared authority, scarce resources and attention: those require a small common book and independent clocks, because separate packages cannot know what the others have just done.

## 2. What is kept in the record

A capsule is a package with a short contents page and attached files; a saved version cannot be edited, and previous versions remain readable. Attachments needed for the next job travel with it, including the relevant source snapshot and actual proof, rather than links that require a departed helper's account. Large unchanged files can be copied once and reused locally, but an exported capsule must include them; credentials and the ability to operate a machine are separate requirements. Par means the budgeted number of times he must respond for a goal to finish.

| Thing | Fields kept | Who may write |
|---|---|---|
| Origin and agreement | Exact words or relevant voice clip and transcript; project suggestion and uncertainty; interpretation; plain done lines; examples; exclusions; delegated choices; reserved choices; spending and change limits; promised showing; par | Capture script preserves origin; counterpart proposes; independent preparer checks completeness; only his answer or an existing standing agreement activates authority |
| Check plan | For each done line: what to observe, input examples, failure examples, method, needed independence, freshness limit, remaining human judgement | Counterpart proposes, separate checker validates before agreement; maker cannot weaken it |
| Applicable rulings | Exact answer, source case, scope, reason, exceptions, expiry, explicit authority versus inferred preference, common-book version, applicability explanation | Answer recorder preserves his ruling; interpreter proposes reusable wording; explicit standing authority needs his words |
| Work and effects | Base versions of things touched; dependencies; resource reservations; bounded next job; candidate files; external action intention and receipt; restore point; unresolved assumptions | Maker appends candidates and observations; restricted action script alone changes shared/live things |
| Checks and outcome | Maker and checker identities; criterion verdicts; independent observations; commands or procedure; input/output fingerprints; source times; dissent; exact accepted version; later defects | Checker writes checks; scripts validate bindings; he accepts or standing acceptance applies within its recorded scope |
| Continuation | Current version; state; next action and its owner; due time; wait condition and fallback; job claim; progress checkpoint; retry limit; stop/amend/revoke/restore events | Transition script enforces allowed changes; counterpart may propose a new plan, never silently move the promise |
| Meter | All jobs, cost and usage; owner displays/answers; cycles and causes; active minutes; words; taps; rule applications; audits; recovery and rework | Wrappers and doorway append observations; helpers can explain causes but cannot erase measurements |

The common book holds each capsule's current recorded version, global preferences and authority, trust by kind of work, due actions, dependencies, resource reservations and the attention budget. Goal-specific entries carry the same immutable event in both places; the book is the ordering authority, not a second editable account. Preferences belong here even before they apply to a goal; the capsule carries the exact subset and reasons it used.
Three record keepers on separate failure domains keep the ordered book; two must agree to accept a write. This is a standard replicated-record requirement, not a proposed homemade file-sync algorithm. A write must name the previous goal version, current authority version and current job claim; stale returns become unaccepted candidates. Any script can resume the role; there is no thinking coordinator, although this storage service must operate.
The current goal version advances only after its capsule and required attachments are durably present on two keepers and their fingerprints match; uploading an incomplete candidate cannot create a ready job. This prevents the common book promising work whose portable evidence disappeared with its maker's computer.
A job claim temporarily assigns one step to one runner; to fence it means rejecting that runner's old claim at the actual write boundary, not merely asking it to stop. A receipt is recorded evidence of what was received or executed, with those two meanings kept separate. Recording a capsule is never the same event as his acceptance of its result.
The all-goals picture is a disposable index of recorded capsule versions. It exposes its last complete observation time and missing machines; a stale view says “unknown since …”, never “nothing needs you”. Rebuilding it requires no helper and no conversation history.
Never store passwords, private sign-in tokens, hidden reasoning, full session transcripts as memory, or unrelated personal data. Keep only evidence necessary for the agreed work, with access restrictions and a retention date; record a missing/redacted proof explicitly rather than inventing a pass.

**One capsule at three moments.** This example fills in an illustrative agreement and answer around the scenario's actual Tuesday fact: Invoice export has six passing lines and two assumptions; the scenario does not supply its detailed tests.
Its unchanged body says: “Invoice export”; six lines: correct period, all permitted invoices, matching totals, correct local dates, one monthly file, and a file that opens on phone and desk. Local-time dates and monthly grouping are declared assumptions, reversible before publication; exporting another customer's data is forbidden.

| Field | Monday, agreed, version 3 | Tuesday 09:00, checked, version 9 | Tuesday 09:23, accepted, version 11 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority and par | His Go on this body; prepare private exports, no public sharing; show Tuesday; cycle 1, par 2 | Same accepted body and authority; no new permission inferred | His Accept binds the exact shown file; cycle 2, par 2 |
| Work and proof | Source snapshot attached; next: build; due 11:00; action claim held | Candidate file attached; six independent passes observed 08:58: boundary dates, permitted-row reconciliation plus forbidden-customer case, cent totals, time-zone boundary, grouping and two-device opening; each has raw output and checker identity | Same six proofs retained; acceptance and observation times separate; no claim about future data |
| Rulings and assumptions | Applicable privacy rule and version copied; local-time dates/monthly grouping listed | Both assumptions shown beside the file; rule versions refreshed before offering it | Acceptance records these choices for this export only; no standing monthly-export permission invented |
| Next action and watch | Build checkpoint by 10:10; if late, reassess/reroute | Show at next sitting; proof valid for this fixed snapshot for 24 hours; stale live inputs trigger recheck | Include in weekly ledger; audit/reopen if defect reported; trust records one pending outcome, qualifying after its defect window |

## 3. The runs

No helper owns the goal between runs. Its next action is the queue; messages merely shorten the delay before a script notices. The following costs are design budgets, not measurements: a script normally takes seconds; a judgement run reads about 2–6 thousand relevant tokens plus any necessary work input. Each run records actual cost, including failures and copied context.

| Job; starts when | Reads → writes | Executor; lifetime; rough effort |
|---|---|---|
| Capture; sentence/voice arrives | Input → immutable origin, suggested routing, receipt | Script; seconds; transcription charged if needed; ambiguity goes into the agreement, not a routing form |
| Agreement; new goal has capacity | Origin, applicable rules, examples → one-minute proposal, authority boundary, checks, exclusions, promise, par | Counterpart helper; 2–5 minutes; one judgement run; request fresh owner intent only when it changes the agreement |
| Prepare/check packet; agreement, sketch or genuine fork is drafted | Origin/contract, actual thing, alternatives, authority, raw evidence → independently checked packet or return to maker | Different helper for semantic review; 2–5 minutes; one judgement run; script checks all required fields |
| Admit/claim/refresh; next action becomes due | Current capsule/book, host capabilities, booked resources and owner time → one job claim, fresh rule bundle, due checkpoint | Script; seconds; no judgement about priority or permission invented here |
| Make; admitted job | Only its bounded task, constraints and necessary attachments → candidate work, observations, assumptions, next checkpoint | Maker helper; at most 20 minutes per chunk; usually 1–2 chunks for medium work; capability-specific actual cost |
| Check; candidate exists | Accepted contract and independent source → reproducible check outputs, counterexample, criterion verdicts and dissent | Scripts for mechanical checks, different helper for judgement; seconds to 10 minutes; normally one judgement run plus test cost |
| Apply/observe/show; checks pass | Candidate, live base version, authority, restore point → action receipt, independent final observation, finite showing | Action and rendering scripts; seconds plus deployment/check runtime; live observations must pass before live success is shown |
| Answer/rule/repair; he responds or checker rejects | Exact answer or failure, shown version → ruling, narrowed rule, reopen/repair/park, next job | Script for structured answers; interpreter for words, usually one 2–5 minute run; a new standing interpretation remains a proposal |
| Trust/audit/admission; outcome, miss or scope change | Qualified outcomes and incidents → trust state, audit selection, reduced/revoked delegated scope, capacity release | Script; seconds; proposing a new delegation needs counterpart judgement, not changing authority by arithmetic |
| Watch/reconcile/restore; clock, restart or discrepancy | Due actions, claims, action receipts, source observations, book health → retry, fence, refresh, restore job, unknown state or changed promise | Independent scheduled scripts; every 2 minutes; no model for counting deadlines or deciding whether proof exists |
| Glance/ledger/cost view; open or weekly boundary | Recorded events and fresh observations → one view, ledger and detailed cost | Script; seconds; unexplained cause stays unknown, not a helper-written success narrative |

Judgement belongs in interpretation, work, ambiguous checks and proposed policy. Deadlines, duplicate suppression, leases, counting, rule filtering and whether a permission exists must never depend on a helper deciding to remember them. A discovered resource or dependency stops admission until declared; integration gets its own check against the combined result.
Before execution the receiver returns a short structured read-back: understood outcome, constraints, next artifact and checkpoint time. A script matches the accepted version and an independent preparer handles substantive mismatch; receipt, understood assignment, candidate work, applied effect and checked outcome are different events. Consequential execution plans get a separate rehearsal, not another owner approval of the same sentence. This borrows the distinct confirmation/execution gates in `research/d7-other-fields.md` without assuming its practices prove attention savings here.

## 4. The gate

One gate controls every owner-facing agreement, question, sketch, showing and incident. A checker can submit directly to it and he can open the original capsule/evidence or revoke its filter; the counterpart cannot hide dissent. A script first requires a current contract, fresh enough facts, independent packet preparation and an explicit consequence/time for waiting; an incomplete request goes back, not to him.

1. **Rule book.** Find applicable, unexpired explicit rulings, narrowest valid scope first; check the cited authority. Resolve the fork and record the rule used when it covers the case. An inferred preference can suggest a recommendation, never grant permission.
2. **Default with a time.** Within already granted authority, a reversible recommendation becomes a recorded assumption and proceeds at its stated time; he need not answer. Outside authority, the default is to preserve the current safe state and park, with a return date or event; elapsed time cannot create consent. He sees the assumption at showing/ledger and can overturn it.
3. **Capacity.** Only a remaining owner-only choice reserves his time. Start with 15 expected minutes in the next sitting and a separate 3-minute urgent reserve; estimate from similar actual reading/answer times, using the slower estimate when uncertain. Also enforce maximum ready age of 24 hours: reprepare, safely default or park an unserved item, rather than silently rolling it forward. These are starting policy choices, not researched optimal limits.
4. **Expiry.** Immediately before offering or acting, recheck need, version, authority, evidence age and latest useful answer time. Withdraw dead questions, cancel their reservations, and record why; record a safe fallback for expired real choices. An old phone answer cannot act on a replaced question.
5. **Next sitting or narrow urgent lane.** Normal items wait for his chosen sitting, ordered by documented cost of delay, then age. Only an owner-exclusive action with an independently verified external deadline before the next sitting may use the urgent reserve and one push; bad planning alone does not qualify. The release sign-in does, and missing the deadline parks/slips the release instead of bypassing sign-in.

Capacity is global, reserved atomically in the common book, including agreements and showings. New work needing eventual review must book a plausible showing window at his observed completion rate; it is parked with a dated capacity check if no window fits. Trusted work that truly needs no owner action can continue within its separate cost/resource limits; many hidden questions are not a way around the budget.
If normal capacity fills, a prepared helper either uses existing authority, joins equivalent decisions into one clear choice, or parks the affected goal. It may not compress the text to understate the minutes. Overlapping urgent cases form one incident choice within the reserve; beyond it, safe fallbacks hold. Increased capacity requires his choice, not a helper's wish to finish more.
Each question shows the actual alternative, recommendation, proof, last useful answer time and “Without an answer, … at …”. The sitting ends when its reserved work ends; unopened leftovers are reassessed on their clock. A changed promise gets one line on next opening, not a push unless the owner must act before then.
The minute budget is an admission estimate, not a guaranteed reading speed: elapsed time is visible, he can stop at any point, and reaching the budget stops offering further items without fresh consent to continue. Time-estimate errors are metered and tighten later bookings; they do not silently redefine the limit.

## 5. The checks

Before Go, each plain done line gets a concrete observation and a failing example. “Reminder texts go out in time” becomes a proposed timing window, time-zone examples, who receives them, and a measured send-to-delivery bound; the person reacts to the proposed behaviour, not a test specification he must write. Unknown timing/provider limits remain explicit discovery work with a commitment limit, rather than a false one-minute agreement.
Scripts check exact properties: invoice membership/totals, access boundaries, artifact fingerprints, timing samples, output format, test results, observation age, and permission to apply the change. They distinguish “maker says”, “observed” and “independently passed”. A test passing for a private candidate does not establish that the changed service runs correctly.
A different helper checks the meaning: do the tests actually cover the plain line, does the work meet the examples, and what realistic case defeats it? It sees the contract and raw source before the maker's rationale, records at least one attempted counterexample and its own observations, and cannot edit the maker's work during that checking run. Same-model correlation remains a risk; a distinct model or specialist is used for consequential ambiguity, and disagreement never disappears into a combined score.
The maker cannot modify the protected check plan or issue a passing receipt; role-limited credentials enforce this. A changed requirement, artifact or relevant source invalidates its earlier passes. Serious disagreement gets another independent investigation, then an owner packet only for a real authority/taste tradeoff; an unresolved correctness failure blocks showing as done.
Only he judges new taste, strategic tradeoffs, consent and whether an ambiguous agreement captures his intent. Show the thing with each line's pass and timestamp, remaining assumptions, source links and dissent available one level down. A checker's signature is evidence of a check, not proof of infallibility; random audits and escaped defects test the checking process itself.

## 6. The watch

Two independently started clock services, on different failure domains, run short scripts; neither is a helper session. Duplicate attempts are harmless because the book accepts one claim per next action. They check each other's last successful end-to-end write/read probe, not a process merely saying it is alive.

| Clock | What trips it | What happens and when |
|---|---|---|
| Work progress | No recorded useful checkpoint for 10 minutes; ordinary chunk due at 20 minutes | Next 2-minute sweep investigates, fences stale writes, requests stop and reroutes isolated work; useful progress is saved output or observed work, not a heartbeat |
| Claim and change reservation | Claim renewed every 5 minutes, expires at 20; reservation names touched thing and base version | Commit checks current claim and base; expiry alone never proves a worker stopped; external changes resume only after fencing or reconciliation proves safety |
| Dependency/wait | Named event absent at its recorded deadline; external replies checked at least daily, with a policy limit, initially 2 working days | Verify the source, try permitted alternative or park with reason and dated return; do not keep asking him to chase an accountant |
| Promise | Remaining booked work plus check/repair allowance no longer fits promised showing; assessed each sweep | Try permitted rerouting once, then record revised date and one promise-change notice; “might still finish” is not a fresh observation |
| Owner choice/default/expiry | Stated default time, 24-hour ready age, or last useful answer time | Re-run gate; act only within authority; otherwise withdraw/park with the exact consequence and next return |
| Parked goal | Its date or dependency fires; fallback date required even for event waits | Revalidate current intent/rules/capacity, reserve a new window and resume; stop/revocation takes precedence |
| Rule and trust | Expiry, changed scope/provider/target, contrary outcome; audits selected before work | Suspend suspect rule or delegation immediately; recheck affected open capsules and already applied cases |
| Observations | Per-check freshness limit exceeded; default live-state limit 10 minutes | Read source again; if impossible mark unknown and block dependent action; a fixed-file proof can last longer because its input is fixed |
| Observer/book/index | No successful write/read probe for 6 minutes, broken replica agreement, missing index entries | Other clock marks observation unknown by minute 8, tries service recovery and exposes last good time; no quiet healthy claim from cached state |
| Delivery and weekly ledger | Accepted event lacks downstream receipt after 2 minutes; weekly boundary arrives | Sweep reconstructs the next action; a push gets one recorded delivery attempt and only a preauthorised alternate after confirmed failure; weekly ledger is assembled, not pushed as a status feed |

An accepted stop cancels future jobs and rejects their returns immediately; actions already in flight are reconciled, not declared undone. Amend creates a new agreement version and invalidates affected checks. Revoke changes shared authority so all dependent claims fail at their next action boundary. Restore is a checked operation from a recorded safe point; restoration itself must be authorised and observed, not assumed possible after an irreversible effect.
Each job has at most two automatic retries within its recorded cost ceiling; a third failure schedules one independent diagnosis, then parks with a concrete blocker and return time if no permitted repair exists. Useful checkpoints cannot extend the chunk deadline indefinitely. Recovery consumes the same resource and authority reservations as ordinary work.
Parked checks are script-only until their triggering facts change or he returns; another date passing is not a reason to pay a helper to reread the same capsule. The book retains the parked obligation even when it is absent from his sitting.
No computer can notice that all computers and communication paths are dead. The doorway has its own cached freshness deadline and displays unknown even offline; an independent external probe is needed to notify during a total local outage. This is a limit of the capsule centre, not a reason to keep a coordinating helper alive.

## 7. Rules and trust

Every answer first becomes an exact ruling bound to the shown version. A proposed reusable rule adds the underlying goal, observable trigger, required action and its check, kind of work, project/surface/role/machine scope, exclusions, reason, precedence, source date, expiry, prior-mistake example and the authority it actually grants. “Accept” settles this goal; it does not become “always do this”. A correction offers “here only / always in this scope” once, on the thing corrected; no answer to that scope question means here only.
Retrieval first filters the book by declared project, kind, object and authority, then uses meaning-based lookup for overlooked matches. A helper explains application when meaning is involved; scripts enforce explicit scope and permission. Each capsule stores the exact matched versions, so the next helper need not remine old conversations. Before commitment, a changed book version refreshes relevant rules; likely-but-uncertain matches become assumptions or checked questions, not silently selected law.
Imported notes and procedures are candidates with provenance, never automatic proof of his permission. At each applicable opportunity record rules considered, rule selected, enforcement check run and outcome; later violations distinguish absent rule, failed retrieval, ignored rule and inadequate check. This answers `research/d3-rules.md`: its extracted candidates and repeated written instructions are not evidence that mistakes stopped, and narrower task/role exceptions must survive importing.
Precedence: current explicit stop/revocation and hard legal/security constraints; his explicit scoped exception; applicable standing authority; narrower explicit preference; broader explicit preference; inferred suggestion. Recency breaks ties only within the same scope and authority. An unresolved genuine contradiction suspends the disputed action; clarification, if only he can supply it, reaches the gate with safe parking as fallback.
A miss quarantines the implicated rule, finds all recorded applications, and schedules impact checks/repair. Expiry or a material context change requires revalidation. The original source answer remains inspectable and can contradict the interpreter; he and a checker can flag that directly without the counterpart's approval.
Trust counts qualified first-time outcomes for a narrow kind plus target/context and risk, not for an individual helper: independent checks passed, no send-back, and no known escaped defect during an initial 48-hour observation window. Count streaks, total attempts, audits, misses, severity and changes; not just acceptance rate. A new provider, target, risk or materially changed check resets the relevant evidence.
After three such outcomes, propose a limited standing agreement: skip the per-goal agreement, keep the showing, par 1. For narrowly defined, reversible wording work he may instead explicitly grant standing acceptance, par 0 on unsampled goals; three successes justify this proposal, not a statistical quality claim. An independent script randomly selects 20% for human audit before starting and reserves their time, par 1 for sampled goals; the maker cannot exclude inconvenient cases, and every result still gets independent machine/helper checks. Irreversible or consequential commitments never earn this timeout/delegation path by streak alone.
A later defect reopens its original goal, preserves acceptance history, revokes the implicated delegation immediately, and restores full showings until repaired checks and fresh outcomes earn a new proposal. Silence or choosing a recommended default is never counted as a first-time human acceptance. Trust changes what he sees, not whether defects can be reported or checks must run.

## 8. The meter

Every event carries goal, version, job, actor role, machine, time, cause and cost; shared work is linked to all benefiting goals with one stated allocation, counted once in totals. The capsule owns its ledger entries and the common book orders them. Allocation also includes storage, script/runner time, record service, idle observation, transcription, setup, recovery and abandoned attempts; unavailable price/usage stays unknown rather than zero.
A cycle is an owner response required to settle a goal's next state: agreement, sketch, fork, correction, parking, acceptance or reopening. Merely opening the glance, reading a notice or seeing an expired question is not a cycle, but its time/words count. One sitting can contain several goal cycles; repeated delivery of one unchanged request is one cycle plus an interruption defect. Par and its reasons are fixed at admission; changes preserve both original and revised par so scope changes cannot erase overrun.
Record causes above par as misunderstanding, avoidable coordination, new discovery, external change, useful dissent, defect or owner scope change; only attributable avoidable causes are waste. Record lead time, parked age, rework and escaped errors beside cycles, so reducing oversight cannot masquerade as improvement.
The doorway measures visible active time, pauses after 60 seconds idle, and separately counts speech duration, actual displayed words, typed/spoken words, taps/keys, notification delivery and surface opened. It labels unobserved thinking/off-device sign-in time as unmeasured and permits a quick correction; it does not claim exact mental minutes. Job wrappers record input/output usage, repeated capsule bytes, work/check/coordination time and billed cost, so rereading and model cost per goal need no later transcript mining.

## 9. Trace the test week

The trace chooses explicit answers only where the scenario leaves them open; it is a design replay, not observed behaviour. Its 15-minute normal budget admits the eight-minute morning sitting; later capacity uses the released time and a booked later sitting.
The Price page is called both owner-only and default-yearly at 5 pm, without a supplied permission record. This trace treats yearly as a recommendation until his morning answer authorises it; without that answer it would park at 5 pm. If earlier authority actually permits yearly, gate step 2 removes the question and the morning needs only two responses. Reversibility alone cannot settle this missing fact.

1. **Morning:** gate assembles three checked packets; he says Go to Driver ratings, Accept to Invoice export and yearly to Price page. Capsules append three responses, release reservations and set next jobs; about eight minutes, three goal cycles, one chosen opening.
2. **12:40 voice:** capture stores his exact reminder-text sentence, proposes the Driving project, and schedules an agreement/check-plan run. A checked agreement comes at his next opening, targeting about one minute; no project form and no push, no expensive/live work before authority.
3. **Seventeen forks:** rule lookup records eleven settlements; three reversible defaults proceed only under existing authority. Three genuine owner-only packets reserve estimated minutes in the next sitting; insufficient room parks their goals instead of expanding a hidden queue.
4. **Wrong welcome sketch:** his “too long, wrong tone” reopens the capsule and records a failed first showing; the attached correction offers “here only / always for welcome emails” once. A chosen scope becomes a ruling, then maker and independent recheck run; no unchosen generalisation.
5. **Not now:** Driver ratings records “after sign-up is accepted”, releases its resources and showing reservation, and keeps a daily fallback checkpoint. The sign-up acceptance event resumes planning; nothing depends on him remembering either goal.
6. **Forty-minute silence:** progress clock trips by minute 12, rejects stale returns and reroutes fenced scratch work. Checkpoints and recovery cost remain in the capsule; the promise holds, so nothing reaches him.
7. **Off all night:** source/host probes detect unavailability, try a capable host, then the promise calculation records the slip. Next opening shows once: “Sign-up shows Friday: its build computer was off overnight”; no separate status interruption.
8. **Sign-in by 3 pm:** independent preparation verifies the deadline and owner-only sign-in; gate uses the urgent reserve and one push linking the secure action. It says “Without sign-in by 3 pm, this release waits a week”; credentials stay with him and the capsule records receipt or slip.
9. **Dead question:** expiry sweep finds its premise superseded, writes withdrawal and releases time. One ledger/next-opening line tells him it was withdrawn; no answer or cleanup task.
10. **Three wording acceptances:** trust job checks distinct outcomes, elapsed defect window and stable scope, then proposes bounded standing acceptance in the next sitting/ledger action. Until he grants it, showings continue; afterwards checks remain and random 20% human audits are booked.
11. **Accepted result wrong:** defect event reopens the same capsule, removes its clean-outcome credit and revokes implicated rule/delegation; a separate helper investigates and repair is checked. A prepared impact/restoration choice reaches him only if his authority or promise must change; the miss is in the ledger.
12. **Three-day return:** first see the last observation time, any unknown coverage or changed promise, then only current owner-only items. Withdrawn/defaulted choices are absent; a prepared sitting is budgeted at 15 minutes, with original evidence one level down, so getting current targets that finite sitting rather than reading three days of reports; actual time and any unfinished work remain visible.
13. **Weekly ledger:** renderer computes done/reopened goals, original/revised par and cycles, measured/unmeasured minutes, decisions delegated, scoped rules, send-backs/causes, questions this/last week and total cost. No helper rewrites the week's history and it is available in the same doorway.
14. **Curiosity:** open the engine view: each current claim's machine/kind, last observation, blocked dependency and cost breakdown, including shared costs. It is read from the book/capsules, never an interruption or an extra place he must maintain.

**Counted replay, with limits:** one work doorway plus the unavoidable external sign-in surface; one intended push, for event 8; zero items deliberately left for him to remember. The three morning responses, further reminder agreement, scoped email correction, three genuine fork answers, parking, trust proposal and sign-in total 11 decision-bearing responses, plus the return sitting's unknown number; each is reserved owner intent, taste or authority after checks. Correction and optional scope are collected in one response, defaulting to local scope. Morning is about 8 minutes, reminder agreement about 1; other minutes, words and taps depend on rendered things and real answers and must be measured, not fabricated from this incomplete week. Admission caps required sitting time; it cannot guarantee that all work is finished in that time.

## 10. What breaks it

| Failure | Catch, bound and safe response |
|---|---|
| Helper stops silently | No useful checkpoint for 10 minutes; sweep catches within 12; fence returns, inspect action receipt, retry a bounded chunk on another capable host |
| Wait never arrives | Every wait has source, owner, fallback and deadline; catch by deadline plus 2 minutes, or next daily external check; alternative/park after policy limit, never an endless “waiting” |
| Message lost | Accepted next action is in capsule/book before sending; 2-minute sweep reconstructs it; stable action key and receipt suppress duplicate effects; uncommitted owner answer displays “not saved” |
| False “done” | Maker cannot set checked/accepted; independent evidence-bound check blocks the next showing; later discovery reopens original goal and audits the checking method; no absolute detection bound for an unknown defect |
| Restart and forgetting | Fresh worker reads contents, contract, current rules and next action; stale claim/version rejected at first write; no session resume is required |
| Same thing changed twice | Global reservation precedes work, isolated candidates avoid shared edits, final write checks original version; missed declared overlap fails integration check; unsupported live targets cannot safely auto-retry an uncertain action |
| Computer off | Independent source probes detect within the 10-minute freshness limit plus one sweep; reroute capabilities if possible; otherwise park and revise promise, as event 7 |
| Record unreachable | No majority means no new claim/authority-sensitive effect; existing copies permit inspection or isolated drafting only; doorway says unknown on freshness expiry, retries when record returns, never creates two authoritative branches |
| Rule wrong | Checker, owner override, source-answer disagreement, audit or defect report quarantines it immediately; find prior uses and repair; expiry bounds stale authority, but an undiscovered wrong rule can survive until its first effective audit |
| Five days without answers | Authorised defaults fire at their times; owner-only goals park at expiry, future review capacity closes new admissions, dated parked checks continue; sign-in release slips safely, no accumulating reminder stream; return is bounded current sitting plus ledger |

Every non-repeatable outside action has a stable action key and an intent recorded before execution. The target must support duplicate suppression or an enforceable current-claim/version check; otherwise an unknown receipt requires inspecting actual state or proving the old actor stopped before retry. Silence or lease expiry alone is insufficient. Reservations and a current-rule check cannot revoke a real-world effect already completed; the capsule must retain that boundary and restoration limits.

## 11. Cost

A medium goal with two maker chunks and a plain acceptance needs five judgement runs: agreement, independent preparation, two maker chunks and independent outcome check; free-text ruling adds a sixth. Showing, recording acceptance, scheduling, clocks and ledger are scripts. A fork adds preparation/check runs only when lookup/defaults fail; rework is charged, never hidden in the baseline.
Budget roughly 20–35 thousand input and 4–8 thousand output tokens across those runs, excluding large source/test work, plus cheap event scripts and an apportioned share of one global sweep every two minutes. These are planning quantities to test, not a price quote. A helper opens the short contents/authority/next-action section first; complete attachments are available without putting all of them into every prompt. Count actual repeated input separately from attachment transfer/storage.
The method's current-record symptom is about 14 coordinator turns for one round, with 68% spent rereading; a round is not a goal, so that cannot establish a savings percentage. This design spends more on independent preparation, packaging, fresh observations, audits and the common book. It should spend less on long coordinator histories, status exchanges, owner explanations and rediscovery after restart; only matched goal replay and live cost/quality measurement can establish the difference.

## 12. Taking part from anywhere

A participant registers its machine, available work kinds, supported check methods, isolation, maximum chunk time and ability to enforce effects safely. It needs only to receive a capsule, inspect attachments, make a candidate change or check, return structured results with source versions/cost, and obey denied/expired claims. A wrapper can do registration, claims and structured receipts for a plain command-line helper; the helper need not understand the whole organisation or speak to another helper.
The capsule's next job selects capabilities rather than a product name. Work can be pushed to a sleeping-machine-independent runner or claimed by a polling worker; identical transitions and receipts are used. A board participates by exposing eligible work/dependencies and receiving mirrored outcomes, not by becoming a second authority; a scheduled service participates as clock, script runner or check executor without pretending to be a judgement helper.
Offline participants can inspect or prepare candidates but cannot establish current shared authority, reserve resources or make irreversible changes. If a needed capability or private sign-in exists on only one host, “any computer” cannot overcome that; the capsule names the requirement and fallback. It carries the handover of knowledge, not transferable credentials or an invented ability to run every tool.

## 13. The smallest first version

Build portable capsule directories, immutable events/check evidence, one transactional common book, a capability/claim wrapper, a due-action script, an independent clock on a second host, and one phone/desk doorway with Say, Sit, Glance and ledger. Include exact origin, authority/default boundaries, explicit scoped rules, one resource reservation per shared target, independent verification, stop/amend/revoke/restore, freshness and the meter from the first real goal. Render showings directly from checked criteria and artifacts; avoid helper-written status copy.
Initially use one durable writer and a read-only replicated backup, not automatic multi-writer failover. The second clock can detect loss, mark unknown and request local service recovery; it cannot promote a new writer without proving the old one fenced. During writer failure progress stops safely. Add majority replication only when recovery measurements justify the operational burden; this first version cannot promise unattended survival of record-host loss.
Leave out inferred preference promotion, automatic trust promotion, broad resource discovery, arbitrary new adapters and cross-host live actions that cannot be fenced. Keep explicit scoped delegation and visible trust statistics; defer reducing review until the audit/recovery path works. Start with two compatible helper wrappers, a read-only board bridge and a scheduled check runner; the shared protocol lets other kinds join without changing goal state.
Estimated build: about 10–15 focused engineering days using existing storage/authentication and runner components, with separate failure-injection and cross-host checks, not a commitment or measured estimate. First checkpoint is one real sentence through checked agreement, isolated work, independent check, showing, scoped answer and ledger; then kill worker, lose delivery, stale rule, concurrent edit and unreachable writer exercises. Compare matched goals for cycles above original par, owner minutes, total cost and escaped defects; target no rediscovery cycle after restart and no owner status-chasing. It is a plausible improvement to today's coordination, not a proved win until those observations exist.

## 14. Three weakest points, method corrections, and existing-tool fit

1. **The centre is no longer pure.** Capsules cannot independently enforce shared authority, resource exclusion, a global attention budget or wake themselves. The common book and clocks are essential infrastructure; majority availability, fencing and honest unknown state are the price of removing the coordinating helper, and the first version deliberately stops on record-host failure.
2. **Correct scope and independent checking remain hard.** A compact capsule can omit the fact that changes the answer; a second helper can share the maker's blind spot. Complete source/evidence, attempted counterexamples, direct dissent and audits reduce this risk without proving absence of error; novel/consequential work still needs specialist or owner judgement.
3. **The attention/cost promise is unproved.** Packaging, fresh reads and preparation can cost more than a warm coordinator on a small goal; duration estimates can hide overload. Meter full recovery/setup effort and defects as well as foreground minutes, and stop admitting work rather than quietly growing the queue.

The method should replace “attention is the only scarce thing” with attention minimisation subject to correctness, harm, cost and recovery limits; permit revisiting agreements as facts change; distinguish a scoped ruling from permanent preference; and distinguish unknown from healthy silence. Seven items is not a capacity argument; budget minutes and age. Not every extra cycle is waste, three acceptances are not statistical trust, and par-zero completion needs explicit standing acceptance rather than an unanswered showing. These changes answer the limits in `research/d5-outside.md`, especially its change column and sections 2/4; this design does not claim that the cited fields validate its thresholds.

**Tool fit, considered only after the design.** Read-only local help established command surfaces, not end-to-end integration; nothing was launched or configured.

|---|---|---|
| Orca-run helpers | Launch capable doers/checkers in isolated workspaces; managed worker identity and status receipts | Capsule/claim adapter; authoritative state remains outside terminal memory; a worker stop or timeout is not proof of external-effect safety |
