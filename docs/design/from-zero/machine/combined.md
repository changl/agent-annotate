# One record, checked work, a prepared doorway

12 November 2026. Revised against method revision 3, both independent replay judgements and the round 2 doorway designs. A separate agreement sitting prevented about two of 416 returns at best and added one per goal; the centre is now cheap checked work shown early. Intervals, allowances and build times remain proposed limits, not verified performance.

## Part 1. The design

### 1. The idea

One record carries his original wish, a say-back prepared as work starts, and every attempt, showing, correction and acceptance.
The say-back supplies the done lines without waiting for him, except for costly, risky, irreversible or genuinely doubtful work; a one-step request needs none.
Short-lived helpers make and independently check the cheapest useful thing that can honestly be shown early, with his decisions gathered beside it.
One doorway keeps the result, original evidence, disagreement and direct controls within reach, while choices outside his authority wait safely.
Build the checked showing and meter first, then earn the watch, shared doorway and greater delegation by reducing his lifetime effort per correctly finished goal.

### 2. Settle the forks

- **Centre: record or office?** The record holds authority; the office determines what reaches him. The cost and failure critics favour the former, the person critic the latter; choosing these separate responsibilities keeps both benefits without a resident chief of staff.
- **One writer or several agreeing writers?** One writer with a tested backup and an outside observer first; accept an honest stop on host loss rather than buy unproved availability machinery.
- **Carry everything or fetch evidence?** Carry the complete accepted purpose and necessary sources in each job; keep large proof once in durable storage, exporting a complete copy only for an actual transfer.
- **How small a run?** One coherent judgement or work slice, not one thought; a helper exits when it deposits its output or declares a bounded wait.
- **Another helper to write the showing?** Render the checked facts and actual result directly; a new interpretation belongs in a checked judgement, not an extra narration.
- **An agreement sitting before work?** No for routine reversible work: record and deliver the say-back as it starts; wait only for cost, risk, irreversibility or real doubt, and omit it for a one-step request. The replay overturns the earlier universal gate.
- **Challenge every say-back in another run?** No; the independent result checker also challenges its done lines against the original wish. Examine a consequential or doubtful plan before committing to it, without delaying a safe cheap experiment on routine wording.
- **Ask early or show early?** Make the cheapest checked sketch, acceptable slice or concrete alternatives first; gather his choices where their consequences can be seen instead of moving them to a separate text approval.
- **Does a moved promise push?** Show it once at next opening unless he must act sooner; the person critic's objection outweighs earlier awareness that buys no useful action.
- **Can an urgent allowance suppress an emergency?** No; reserve time, merge duplicate consequences, but expose every distinct loss that passes the interruption test, counting any overrun.
- **Which clocks and load allowance?** Use a minute sweep, ten-minute useful-progress limit, twelve-minute sitting including two urgent minutes, and one-working-day ready-age guard; measure and revise these settings rather than maintain competing regimes.
- **Three, five, or automatic trust?** Three acceptances can prompt the scenario's proposal; five comparable matured outcomes are the initial evidence floor for a grant, never an automatic promotion or reliability claim.
- **Two-day or seven-day maturity?** Name the relevant defect window in the checking plan; start at seven days unless a different observed-use window is justified, rather than treating two quiet days as universal proof.
- **Who pays for samples?** Independently select staff audits first; require human samples only for a named judgement gap in his grant, reserving their minutes before work. A compulsory human sample of every kind would recreate his checking job.
- **How many states?** Six: preparing, working, awaiting him, parked, done, stopped; proof, delivery, health and trust are separate facts, not multiplied goal states.
- **Which baseline and cycle unit?** Use the reconciled baseline and one submitted return per goal, defined in section 11; retain finer asks and decisions so batching cannot hide effort.
- **Full machine or useful slice first?** Stage 1 is the checked showing, meter and non-blocking say-back; stage 2 the watch/glance; stage 3 the record across helpers, one doorway, defaults and gathered decisions; stage 4 rules/trust. The replay removes a stand-alone agreement stage.

### 3. The record

Every entry has one permanent goal identity, its own receipt, writer and role, observed and received times, source, cause, preceding version, agreement version and authority used. A correction adds a replacement link; it does not overwrite the earlier fact. Retrying the same receipt returns the original acknowledgement. A content mark identifies the exact saved contents of a result or source.
The same goal identity accompanies board items, assignments, handovers, requests, proof and later defects. Splitting work creates linked jobs, not new goal identities that erase returns. A genuinely new desired outcome gets a new linked goal; scope changes retain the old terms, par and cause. An agreement version means the recorded working terms, not proof that he approved them; his actual permission and any required go are separate receipts.

One writing service enforces roles, expected versions and current permission at every shared change. Helpers have private work areas and cannot rewrite admitted checks, publish directly or accept their own output. To fence an attempt means rejecting its former permission at the shared-write boundary, not proving its process stopped. Current views are disposable readings of the record with a last-processed position; authority-sensitive actions recheck the authoritative versions.
Keep necessary source snapshots and proof under access and retention limits. Keep credentials, private reasoning and unrelated conversations out. Removing sensitive evidence leaves a removal receipt; a check whose necessary evidence disappeared becomes unknown.

### 4. The runs

The judgement packet is exact: original wish and any question it answered; complete current say-back/working terms and done lines; checking plan; actual permission receipts; applicable rule versions with source cases and exceptions; relevant source/result contents or durable references; dependency/resource versions; last failure and dissent; one requested output; checkpoint, finish and spending limits. Nothing relies on a predecessor's conversation. A one-step request is itself the done-line source and gets no separate say-back. Inspect available context before asking about a referent; missing evidence becomes a named record request, not a guessed history or automatic owner question. Large inputs need a budgeted reading job or a narrower slice without dropping a constraint.

Jobs are merged by goal, kind and input version. Receipt loss cannot create a second logical job. A recipient's substantive read-back mismatch returns to preparation before effects; familiar read-back occurs within its work run, without a separate approval ceremony.
Preparing normally leads straight to working within the request and existing authority, with the say-back delivered and editable. Only costly, risky, irreversible or genuinely doubtful work awaits the necessary go; an existing exact go is not asked again. Working includes checking and making the first useful showing as early as possible, then his gathered choices, acceptance, authorised completion or park. A changed line updates the terms, not automatically a new approval sitting; stop ends admission and a defect reopens the original goal. No helper remains alive awaiting a person or date.

### 5. The gate

Apply this order on new facts, deadlines, opening and again on an answer; record the first reason that settles an item.

1. **Validate.** Honour stop/revocation; require current terms and actual authority, source-grounded done lines, fresh necessary observations and relevance. Classify costly/risky/irreversible work or real doubt for required go; an ordinary say-back and a one-step request create no approval task. Merge duplicates, reject stale answers and withdraw dead questions. Dissent cannot be rejected because it disagrees with preparation.
2. **Use the ruling.** Apply current case instruction, then an unambiguous applicable standing authority with its exceptions. Record the rule and enforcement check. Preference may rank a recommendation; it cannot supply permission. Unresolved conflict holds only the disputed effect for bounded interpretation.
3. **Use one of two defaults.** An action default proceeds only within a prior explicit grant, with reversible effect, tested undo, target and spending limits and a named time. Otherwise, at its stated deadline, the default preserves the current safe state and parks, with owner, consequence and return; before then a valid owner choice may occupy a sitting. It never accepts a new goal. Already delegated implementation choices need no courtesy question.
4. **Reserve minutes and bound age.** Begin with twelve predicted minutes per sitting, two reserved for urgency; count exceptional go decisions, showings with their gathered choices, taste, policy, audits and recovery. Book expected viewing within the next two sittings before discretionary work starts. Above capacity, recommend a concrete stop-list and hold lower delay-cost work with a return. One working day after first readiness, any unserved item must default within authority or park explicitly; rewriting cannot restart its age.
5. **Revalidate expiry and urgency.** Before display or timed action, recheck need, authority, proof and last useful answer time. The sole interruption passes all three: only he can act; it cannot wait for his next sitting; a named, independently supported loss follows. No permitted substitute may avert that loss. Every push is counted as an interruption and in the method's miss ledger, with its cause, including necessary private sign-in.
6. **Serve the sitting.** Order by evidenced cost of delay, then first-ready age. Gather his unresolved choices into the earliest honest showing of the actual result or cheap concrete alternatives, with recommendation, uncertainty and “Without an answer, … at …”. Do not hold a useful sketch for polish or a promised later date. Only a necessary go or a decision blocking even a cheap safe experiment arrives without such work to look at. End at the allowance; he can pull more. Never hide a distinct urgent loss to meet an interruption target.

Related notices share one delivery identity; retrying delivery is not a fresh notification. At the deadline, lack of acknowledgement takes the stated safe fallback. A moved promise, withdrawn question or contained repaired defect waits for next opening unless it passes the same three-part test. Infrastructure failure has no automatic exception: staff attempt bounded recovery, stop unsafe autonomy and show “I cannot see”; contacting him requires the same actionable-loss test.

### 6. The checks

For every done sentence in the say-back, or the original one-step request, record its source, observable example, deliberately failing example, method, conditions, target, necessary proof, remaining human judgement and freshness. Record and deliver routine terms as work starts: his silence neither approves invented assumptions nor removes the request's existing authority. Before costly/risky work, another helper asks “Could something useless pass?” For routine work that challenge belongs in the independent result check. Unknown intent buys the cheapest checked sketch, experiment or concrete alternatives, with decisions beside them; only real doubt that blocks safe preparation needs a prior go. Do not invent “diagnosis only”, “no execution” or other exclusions he did not give.

Security, speed, real accounts and physical devices add checks to every applicable kind. A provider's send receipt does not prove phone delivery or attendance; a screenshot does not prove interaction; a faster function does not prove a faster complete journey. Use qualified specialist judgement where general helpers cannot adequately check.
Each pass binds the exact result, agreement/check versions, source inputs, target/build/account/device, method, environment, observed time and independent writer. Changed contents or assumptions invalidate affected passes; integration gets fresh shared-boundary checks. A private candidate passing is separate from the installed/served effect passing. The maker cannot weaken the plan or write a pass; the checker cannot fix and approve its own change.
The showing lists **not checked**, line by line: skipped cases, unavailable hardware/accounts, excluded environments, stale observations and unresolved assumptions, with consequence and next step. Unknown is never included in the passing count. Only a clearly labelled diagnostic sketch may reach him before correctness checks pass; its purpose is a reserved reaction, not acceptance as finished.
Introduce or change a check by first demonstrating failure on the old/broken result or a planted bad control; include clean controls to measure false objections. Select outcome audits independently and preserve dissent in the checker's own words. A signature or second model alone is insufficient independence; source-first examination and another method matter.
Immediately before an irreversible act, pause on fresh actual state and consequential assumptions. Show the exact rendered content/result, recipient or audience, amount, target, authority and short expiry; executor reads these back and he confirms that bound act. Changed facts invalidate the confirmation. Execute once under the current permission, then observe the real outcome; an unknown receipt demands reconciliation before any retry. Refund, retraction or rollback is a separately authorised remedy and cannot erase exposure.

### 7. The watch

Progress means a new saved result, independently observed changed state, or more done lines passing for that result. Process life, elapsed effort and repeated “alive” buy no progress credit. Checkpoints are proposed before the run; a genuinely slow operation names its observable boundary rather than producing artificial files to renew time.

| Clock | Initial trip and action |
|---|---|
| Intake/delivery | No durable receipt in 30 seconds: retain locally and reconcile/retry the same identity; unclaimed job after two minutes: offer elsewhere if safe |
| Claim | Five-minute publishing permission, local script renews each minute only within the progress and finish limits; reject expired permission at the actual shared-write boundary |
| Useful progress | Ten minutes without the promised durable advance; minute sweep detects by minute eleven, observes effects and fences the attempt; one replacement attempt, then one bounded diagnosis and park |
| Run and spend | At the declared finish, normally at most 30 minutes, or reserved usage/money ceiling: stop new tools, request termination and observe exit; rejected publishing rights alone do not stop billing |
| Dependency/park | Named event or fallback date, initially no later than next working day; observe source, use permitted alternative or park with next return; daily reconciliation catches a lost event |
| Promise | Every material change and five minutes: remaining work/check/repair allowance no longer fits; reroute within budget or record revised promise and reason once |
| Default/question/load | Exact deadline, relevance change or one-working-day ready age: run gate; act, withdraw or park; admission also reserves future review minutes |
| Live proof/source | Named validity boundary, initially ten minutes for live state: reobserve or mark unknown; immutable-file proof lasts only while its identified inputs and assumptions hold |
| Rule/outcome/sample | At each use and due observation; daily overdue scan and weekly audit: suspend expired/conflicting authority, mature outcomes or reopen misses |
| Watch, record and doorway | Independent host probes real read/write and a harmless due action each minute; missing two successive receipts or due action exposes failure by minute three; doorway expires cached health itself, even offline |
| Backup/recovery | Copy committed record, necessary proof and its inventory every five minutes; verify saved position and exercise restoration before admitting work; missing tail or proof remains quarantined |
| Accounts/retention/worker exit | Weekly ledger; named evidence retention deadlines; at settlement release reservations and end owned disposable workers unless explicitly retained for another purpose |

The outside observer must see a harmless action becoming due and actually completing, not just a fresh heartbeat or healthy web address. It watches source coverage and the watch's processing position. After sleep/restart, sweep overdue work before admission; retries and diagnoses share the original goal's budget.
The glance distinguishes reported, observed, checked, held and unknown, with last observation and next return. Missing coverage says **“I cannot see the work. Last seen …”**, and lists what is blind. “Nothing needs you” means no current required owner action, not that every result is correct; held commitments and their dates remain visible.
Reserve system-wide spending atomically as well as per-goal spending; include concurrent jobs, retries and audits. Do not release a running process's spend reservation until exit or a provider stop is observed; uncertainty closes further admission. Name an operating owner and bounded recovery procedure before the pilot; unresolved maintenance becomes a prepared incident, with his recovery time counted if only he can act.
Loss of record access blocks new claims and shared effects immediately; authorised private preparation can finish within its budget. An independent observer can expose loss, but cannot manufacture current authority. If all hosts and communication fail, timely notification is unbounded; cached health still expires. A backup takeover requires proving the old writer fenced and reconciling actual effects, never declaring a second writer because it is reachable.

### 8. Rules and trust

An answer first settles its exact case. Offer “here only, or future cases in this scope?” once beside a correction; no scope answer means here only. An unambiguous explicit wider instruction needs no repeated permission; inferred preference can guide a recommendation but never grant an effect.
A wider rule carries the underlying goal, observable trigger, action and enforcing check, work kind, project/surface/role/machine scope, exceptions, authority boundary, reason, source/date, prior-mistake example, precedence and expiry. Retrieve it at agreement, dispatch, fork and action by those conditions, preserving contrary cases. At commitment refresh changed authority; record rules considered, selected, enforced and outcome, including “none”. Review advisory preferences initially every thirty days; permission ends with its goal unless a standing grant names its duration. Staff may revalidate unchanged advice, never extend permission through a review date.
Current stop/revocation and explicit case exception win; then explicit supersession and unambiguous applicable scoped authority. Newer alone does not beat a different scope. A genuine unresolved conflict parks that effect, not every project. A miss compares source ruling with its interpretation, suspends the suspect rule, finds every application and rechecks the affected chain. Preserve the original and replace/retire the wrong version; distinguish missing rule, missed retrieval, ignored rule and inadequate check.
Trust belongs to kind plus context, target, risk and checking method. Count independent first-pass acceptances, corrections before closure, later defects, audits and sufficient observed use. Three wording acceptances permit a proposal; the initial grant floor is five relevant outcomes surviving their agreed window, working fault controls and independent audits. Staff prepare one bounded grant and its expected review cost; he grants it explicitly, or full viewing continues.
Start random staff re-examination at one in five comparable results, selected before work by a script the maker cannot control. Add a preselected human sample only for a stated taste/intent uncertainty staff cannot resolve; the grant names its initial rate and reserves minutes. If that burden cancels the benefit, do not widen delegation. No result loses its ordinary independent checks, and no failure disappears because it was unsampled.
First candidates are J test-only work, G bounded local tools, L archived reversible cleanup and C settled mechanical wording. Next are familiar B web/F service repairs, H read-only or repeat-safe jobs, I private installs and E source-exact reversible records, with their own target/undo evidence. A native/car work needs a separate physical-evidence track. D new styles, A/B new journeys, C new voice and K new direction retain personal reaction; mechanical variants may later qualify narrowly.
A grant may omit routine showings: par zero for covered unsampled work, with sampled human reactions booked and counted. The non-blocking say-back is already free of a required response; trust is not needed to remove that ceremony and cannot waive a genuinely required go. Irreversible money, messages, disclosure, destruction and public releases never gain permission from kind-level trust. Any material miss or changed target/risk/check context suspends the implicated freedom, restores full viewing, traces prior uses and requires fresh evidence plus a new grant; unrelated kinds keep theirs.

### 9. The way back

Stop, change the agreement, take back permission and undo sit beside the result and have a direct route that does not require a preparing helper. A control receipt says which future effects were blocked, which processes actually stopped and which effects are already complete or uncertain. A control saved offline is labelled not yet shared; it cannot claim remote work stopped.
Changing the agreement suspends incompatible work and invalidates affected checks; it does not silently narrow the original done lines. Undo starts from a saved safe point, observes today's target, checks preservation and the inverse independently, then acts only within existing restoration authority. If no safe inverse exists, show the actual loss and a prepared remedy choice.
A later defect reopens the original accepted goal and preserves its acceptance, cycles and cost. Follow every recorded reliance on that exact version through downstream results and releases; quarantine affected pending effects, identify already exposed targets/recipients and examine uncertain links. Repair the source, then rebuild and check dependents in dependency order. Compensation for released damage has its own authority and proof. Release quarantine only when each affected branch is checked sound or explicitly held with an owner and consequence; fixing the first file does not close the incident.

### 10. Rhythm and absence

He chooses sittings and can pull a prepared item early from the same doorway. The say-back is recorded and delivered quietly there as work starts, without a push, a sitting or a required response. The sitting shows estimated minutes, offers checked work with its gathered decisions and located correction, plus go only for an exception, and ends with “That is all” plus explicit held commitments and next return. “First look” is the earliest useful checked experiment or slice, not a promise to wait until the whole goal is polished. He never approves staff accounts merely to clear them.
“Goes ahead at 5 pm” is a recorded execution time inside authority already given, independent of whether he opens the 5 pm sitting. If a last look was promised, offer it before that time. Yearly-first pricing therefore needs an existing grant for that reversible choice; otherwise 5 pm means park, even though the test week calls yearly a default. A truly reserved choice cannot simultaneously be a permission inferred from silence.
“Not now, after sign-up” parks ratings on sign-up's accepted or legitimately delegated completion, with a next-working-day source check; it releases work and viewing reservations. An accountant wait similarly names its owner, return source, date and fallback, without a live helper waiting.
During five days away, authorised reversible defaults continue within limits; owner-only and irreversible branches take their stated hold, and new work needing his judgement stops as reservations fill. Trusted no-view work may continue inside cost/resource limits. Questions that died are withdrawn; genuine obligations retain consequences and tested returns rather than disappearing with a retired page.
On return, first reconcile, then show observation coverage and changes since his last visit: uncontained harm, missed commitments, defaults, held consequences and recommended next actions. Allow about two of the twelve minutes for orientation, then current choices; unresolved work remains explicit for later sittings. “Current” means he understands the consequences, not that every accumulated decision can honestly be finished in twelve minutes. Original papers and the weekly ledger remain available without reading the intervening conversations.

### 11. The meter

**Frozen cycle rule:** after the unsolicited first statement, one submitted owner action concerning a goal counts as one return: agreement, reaction, correction, acceptance, question/nudge, relay, park, stop or reopening. One page submission counts once per goal touched, regardless of cards; separate chat submissions count separately. A submission touching two goals counts once on each, but once in total physical contacts. Opening, unsubmitted drafts, passive notices, automatic defaults, timeouts and unviewed requests add no cycle; their exposure, age, decisions and cost are recorded separately.
Record raw requests, decisions, displays, submits, timeouts, withdrawals, versions and source identities, so alternative analyses remain possible without changing this pilot rule. A correction appended to an acceptance and sent together is one return; an extra later correction is another. Late reopening continues lifetime totals. Closed-board status, a helper's finish and acceptance of one subpart never substitute for explicit acceptance of the bounded result.
Freeze original par when the say-back and done lines are recorded: one per independently acceptable slice, plus each genuine taste showing, plus one only when the say-back had to wait for his go. A one-step request uses its original done condition without a say-back. Trusted unsampled completion is zero; a required sample gets its declared showing allowance. Preserve the parent total and original scope; record revised par without erasing the original or renumbering historical cycles. Each cycle has one evidenced primary cause and supersession link: planned owner decision, intent miss, quality miss, unnecessary question, changed wish, status/nudge, relay, tooling, useful discovery, external change or necessary authority; uncertain remains unknown. Only attributable avoidable causes are misses; interruptions also retain the separate miss flag. Par is a comparison, not a promise that decisions and discoveries disappear.
The doorway records foreground active seconds, pauses after 60 idle seconds or loss of focus, words actually displayed, typed and spoken words, taps/keys, required destinations, notifications and submissions. Initial wishes, policy/audit/ledger time, recovery and outside sign-in time count; off-screen thinking and uncovered time are explicitly unmeasured, never zero or inferred from answer latency. Pointing and voice corrections need no report written by him.
Wrappers record actual input/cached/output usage, elapsed execution, repeated packet/source versions, tool/compute charges, model and machine. Include failed/discarded attempts, check/rule upkeep, backups, setup and maintenance. Keep actual bills distinct from list-price estimates and unknown charges. Allocate shared overhead once by active goal-days, disclose the rule, and show setup separately rather than losing it outside the goal comparison.
Per goal derive cycles/original and current par; request-to-acceptance days including parked time; his measured/missing minutes and words; interruptions; questions; misses/discoveries; first-pass checks; later defects and total cost. Weekly show completed and reopened goals, unfinished age, delegated choices, learned/revoked rules, causes and questions this/last week. Fewer questions bought by holding more goals is visible, not a success claim.
The reconciled baseline is 759 observed returns across 80 paths, not revision 2's older 774; only 16 have both clear opening and bounded acceptance, with median three returns. The comparable A/B/C/F stratum and dominant-goal D/E stratum stay separate. Active historical minutes and exclusive per-goal costs are missing; no savings percentage can be manufactured from them.

### 12. Walk the 87

These are the primary kinds in the stall study, not 87 live broken workers. Detection limits are proposed; semantic omissions have no guaranteed bound. Successful recovery is silent only while its commitment holds.

| Recorded kind / cases | Catch and time | What he sees |
|---|---|---|
| Silent stop / 8 | Provider failure at return, or no useful progress by minute eleven; fence, reconcile and replace once | Nothing if recovered; otherwise next-opening hold or changed promise, urgent only under the test |
| Waiting on nobody / 2 | Reject wait lacking owner/event/date immediately; source recheck at return, normally next working day | Named hold and consequence, never a request to chase staff |
| Lost message / 9 | Durable next action, 30-second receipt reconciliation, two-minute pickup check; recipient read-back and version checks | “Not yet shared” for unsaved input; otherwise only material consequence |
| False finish / 14 | Independent actual-delivery-path check before showing; later use/audit for concealed faults, without a guaranteed discovery time | Actual checked result or explicit unknown; a later miss gets consequence/remedy and reopening |
| Lost memory/instruction / 5 | Complete accepted packet and rule refresh on takeover/dispatch; read-back before effects | No re-explanation of recorded constraints; genuinely missing evidence is named |
| Drift / 15 | Source-linked done-line coverage at dispatch and non-maker check at first artifact compare with original complete scope | Corrected work; only genuine new intent/taste comes back |
| Collision / 4 | Reserve shared objects before dispatch; isolated candidates; current-base/permission check at publication and integrated checks | Staff resolve conflict; only a real priority or changed promise needs him |
| Machine trouble / 14 | Failed operation immediately; missing coverage by minute three; reroute or hold unique-hardware work | Honest coverage/hold and revised promise; no instruction to restart workers |
| Premature handback / 7 | At each result, check every authorised done line and the next action; incomplete ready work is dispatched again | Checked completion or concrete external blocker, never a decorative finish |
| Stale tracking / 3 | Reconcile goal, job, actual result and board on transition/next minute sweep | One current truth with dates; mismatched tracking cannot certify completion |
| Blocking question / 1 | At preparation, convert it to a recorded branch wait; release helper and dependent reservations | Its reserved choice beside useful work at a sitting; unrelated releases continue |
| Resource starvation / 2 | First admission refusal records holder/return; two-minute pickup check and claim expiry; budget bounds retries | Explicit hold/stop-list if it changes his priorities |
| Excessive wakeups / 1 | Empty/unchanged sweeps launch no helper; duplicate notices collapse immediately to one consequence; usage flags recurring empty runs | No heartbeat stream or 472 alarms; one actionable incident if needed |
| Needless scheduled wait / 1 | Readiness event starts authorised work immediately; calendar is only a fallback wake-up | Work advances now; he may pull a showing early |
| Finished workers retained / 1 | At settlement release owned resources and exit disposable run; explicit retention is distinguished | No cleanup chore; any retained purpose is visible below the doorway |

“Waiting on him without telling him” was not established by the study and is not added to the 87. The design still requires every such wait to have delivery, deadline, consequence and return. Board age, 232 failed reports and 64 blocked tasks are not interchangeable stall evidence; an honest failed check is often the machine working correctly.

### 14. Stages

The replay supersedes the old ordering: the 59 messages labelled as agreement opportunities did not become prevented whole returns when the agreement was tested blind. It prevented about two of 416 at best; checks, watch, record, defaults and one doorway supplied the conditional whole-method gain. Remove the stand-alone agreement stage, put its non-blocking checklist in stage 1, move watch/glance from stage 3 to stage 2, gather cross-helper record/doorway/defaults/decisions in stage 3, and leave wider rules/trust last. A minimal durable record and actual permission checks exist from day one; stage 3 extends them across helpers. Before each stage starts, pass its paper tests; exercise the same faults against the real boundary before widening scope.

**Stage 1 — checked showing, meter and non-blocking say-back; target two to four focused engineering days to first real use.** Use one existing launcher and mechanical checks, but build a new small showing sheet from the round 2 design, with original-wish capture, a say-back delivered and recorded as work starts, source-linked done lines, exact-result/check receipts, non-maker checking/repair, located correction/acceptance, minimal accounts and stop. A one-step request uses its own done condition without say-back. Start with private reversible reports, test repairs or settled wording, one slice at a time; no autonomous publishing or trust policy. Keep a bounded deadline and outside freshness check as pilot safety. A doubtful/costly/risky/irreversible proposal takes the exceptional go path; never create easier done lines or fabricated exclusions to avoid it.
At the end he can state the goal once, leave routine work to start, then inspect the earliest useful independently checked result and point at a fault; failed checks return to repair before he becomes the checker. This replaces maker-authored “done”, a routine go round and owner-discovered rework. Meter from his original sentence, including any genuine prior approval; showings use the new sheet, not a reused review route. A preparatory sketch is honestly labelled with what is and is not checked.
Before starting: use the judged replay to test non-blocking versus required-go cases, a card answer with its initiating question and a one-step move. Blind-check old/broken candidates, wrong total, stale served build, missing device proof and wrong tone with clean controls; reject false done. Recount fake submits, a passive say-back, idle time, one paragraph and late reopening. Walk kill/lost receipt/old result/stop/backup-gap cases: no unauthorised effect or false health. Point to an actual checkable defect this path would keep from reaching him; do not claim agreement credit for repeating a line he already supplied.
For acceptance, enrol at least six comparable pairs of real bounded goals before results are known, alternate today's route and the checked route, preserve outcome criteria and independently assess final quality. Count repairs, setup, waiting, unresolved goals and seven-day escaped defects. The checked group must lower median lifetime returns to bounded acceptance by at least one, reduce owner minutes, and add no escaped defect or unobserved stall; report every pair, not only completed successes. If absent, stage 1 has not earned expansion. Historical median three on clearly bounded accepted paths and new par one are context, not proof of the gain or a promise for discovery work.

**Stage 2 — the watch and the glance.** Add the general due index, useful-result clocks, bounded recovery, dependency returns, independent due-action observer, fresh/partly blind new glance and since-last-visit account. He can leave work and return to dated truth and prepared consequences without checking processes or computers. This replaces heartbeat turns, manual nudges, status summaries and owner-managed restarts. Keep shared effects excluded until their actual permission boundary is proved; the glance follows the new sitting/letter/return designs, not an existing inbox.
Before starting: replay all stall kinds and the fourteen scenario events on paper; kill/stuck/late worker, lost answer/result, sleep, lost writer and checked restore; healthy probe while due work stops; cached doorway offline; dependency park/return and several genuine deadlines. Replay forty goals and three/five days away, retaining every held/lost consequence. Another reader must understand the return account without reconstructing the week. Measure script-first detection, false stalls, recovery, freshness coverage, unknown time, nudges, promise changes, return understanding and full maintenance cost. **Stage 3 — record across helpers, one doorway, defaults and gathered decisions.** Extend the same record/packet and goal identity across Orca helpers, Claude Code, Codex, Beads and Windmill as their boundaries pass. Carry whole purpose/checks/actual authority through cold pickup; gather his choices into early showings with concrete alternatives instead of sending separate preliminary questions. ### 15. Running both ways

Adopt one project and new bounded goals first; other projects continue normally. For existing work, attach the known original wish, current authority and actual result without pretending an old closure was acceptance. Unknown history remains unknown. A goal enters by a recorded boundary with one route for his answers and one owner of its next action; no obligation is asked in both old and new surfaces.
Initially mirror proposed transitions without effects or extra owner questions, compare them to today's real decisions, then enable the new checked-showing sheet for that project. Import necessary artifact/board data behind the new doorway; no old review or inbox viewer becomes part of what the migrated project asks him to use. Reconcile missing/duplicate receipts mechanically. The record owns goal authority/proof, the board owns its agreed task fields, and runner/scheduler receipts remain observations. Retired cards get a withdrawal or transferred-obligation reference, not silent disappearance.
At every stage he can stop migration: fence new dispatch, reconcile in-flight effects, export current agreement/result/check/next-action packets, restore that project's former answer route and continue work there. This is rollback of the route, not erasure of meter history. Move the next project only after the previous one meets its stage measure and its specific kinds of proof and undo work.

### 16. Cost

A routine medium goal with six done lines and two work slices uses four judgement runs: prepare the non-blocking say-back/check packet, two makers, one integrated-result checker that also challenges coverage. A consequential or genuinely doubtful plan adds a separate challenge and any necessary owner go; neither is a universal stage. One-step work needs no say-back run. Scripts render/routinely route/record/count, roughly 12–30 grouped invocations plus shared clocks; repairs, sketches, new decisions, audits and upkeep are additional charged work.
[R's packet runs](../research/r-replay-writing.md) measured complete response processes at median 20.3, 17.6 and 18.4 seconds for its three ways, not an isolated startup charge or a general work-runtime guarantee. For planning, assume 2 + 15 + 15 + 5 = 37 useful helper-minutes, plus a conservative twenty-second call allowance per run and one to three script minutes: about 39–41 active minutes serially, 24–26 with safely parallel makers. A separate challenge adds about two minutes plus its call allowance; required go adds actual waiting. These useful-work and additive allowances are assumptions, not measured medium-goal timings; packet inference may overlap them. Today's comparable active wall time is unknown, so no speed saving is claimed.
The [Sol](../research/r-replay-judge-sol.md) and [Opus](../research/r-replay-judge-opus.md) clean-sentence judgements put agreement alone at 452 or 453 returns against 416 actual across 36 goals: one found two prevented and none moved up, the other none prevented and three moved up. Their whole-method scenarios leave 242 or 227 returns, respectively, conditional on checks/watch/record/defaults/doorway actually working. Those are remaining returns, not returns saved; the old agreement sitting is still inside their arithmetic. Do not subtract its 36 sittings mechanically to claim a validated forecast for this revision. Many paths are censored, sixteen include initial statements, retrospective seeds can leak hindsight, and no owner minutes or live counterfactual costs were measured.
E's effort study attributes 27.1% of allocated tokens to helper coordination and 26.1% to reading/re-reading; neither is removable waste. Its separate four-producing-worker sensitivity has a $17.01 notional baseline: two short coordinator runs cost $17.57 with no saving, $16.40 at assumed 25% non-startup saving and $15.24 at 50%; four at 25% already cost $17.52. That is not a bill for this different four-run design. X's earlier tiny runs used about 13.9 thousand input tokens each; they do not price R's twenty-second packets. Actual subscriptions, normal Claude latency and full charges remain unknown. Count real usage, maintenance, wall time and owner effort per accepted goal; never compare runs with fourteen historical turns per round as if they shared a unit.
The remaining roughly half consists substantially of his own decisions, taste and discoveries. Early checked showings and gathered choices may make these cheaper, but no replay establishes that their number or minutes shrink. Budget for them, measure their cost and held-work consequences, and keep throughput honest when his judgement remains the limiting resource.

### 17. What could make this fail

The replay's favourable whole-method scenarios still leave 227–242 of 416 returns. Roughly twenty-second packet runs make short jobs affordable to try; they do not prove that either those remaining decisions or the surrounding operating effort becomes smaller.

| Likely failure | Earliest sign and response |
|---|---|
| The remaining half of returns does not shrink | Correct checked early work still needs new taste, choices and discoveries; gather them beside the result and measure minutes/throughput, without relabelling them as defects, hiding bad news or promising universal par |
| A checker merely agrees with the maker | A planted fault passes, a wrong served build is accepted, or verdict has no independent observation; disqualify that check method and repair before more showings |
| The quiet system cannot observe itself | Harmless due action stops while health remains green, stale coverage survives expiry, or record loss permits publication; stop autonomy and repair observation/enforcement before resuming |
| Permission, a rule or an accepted defect escapes its boundary | A default lacks a source grant, an old claim still writes, or an affected downstream release is absent from impact tracing; quarantine scope, reconcile actual effects and require fresh checked authority |
| The office costs more attention and effort than it saves | Stage 1 fails its one-cycle win, starts/audits/policy consume the saving, or held-goal age grows while the front looks empty; cut ceremony or stop expansion, with lost throughput and full cost visible |

This is a documentary design only. No runtime, phone-delivery, confinement, failure-recovery or savings claim has been verified here; those are explicit stage gates, and unknown intent and correlated failure retain unbounded discovery risk.
