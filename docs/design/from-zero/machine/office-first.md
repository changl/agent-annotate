# The machine as a private office

## 1. The idea

The office receives each wish into one in-tray, prepares one paper saying what is asked and what silence will cause, and carries the signed instruction into an out-tray for execution. A bring-forward file, standing orders, independent examiners and a duty clerk keep work moving without a permanent chief of staff. The principal gets a short briefing book when he sits down, while the original papers, dissent and the age of every observation remain open to inspection.

## 2. What the record keeps

One authoritative register accepts additions, checks permissions and preserves earlier versions; its current views are rebuilt from those additions. Every entry has a reference, goal, author, time, source, preceding version and authority used; references stay behind the person's plain words. Helpers submit claims; they cannot rewrite instructions, their own checking verdicts, or the clerk's observed state.

The agreement's front paper targets 120 words: what I heard, done, delegated/reserved choices, exclusions and next showing, with the proposed action already selected for acceptance. Sources and examples open underneath. If that cannot honestly carry the uncertainty in about a minute, offer a bounded discovery or sketch agreement first; do not bury a larger commitment in fine print.

| Paper | Fields beyond the common fields | Who may add it |
|---|---|---|
| Incoming wish and agreement | Original words; interpreted purpose; constraints; numbered done lines; examples; exclusions; delegated decisions and spending; reserved decisions; assumptions; next showing; par; acceptance | Intake clerk records words; briefing helper drafts; different examiner checks; principal accepts or amends |
| Work order | Agreement version; bounded task; inputs and their versions; dependencies; exclusive resources; allowed effects; read-back; worker requirements; budget; checkpoint; return time; current holder | Planning helper proposes; clerk admits; recipient adds read-back |
| Receipt and observation | Assignment; attempt; observed process or artifact; observed-at; received-at; source; next observation; reported/observed/checked distinction | Machine clerk observes; helper may report, explicitly labelled |
| Decision submission | Exact fork; evidence and dissent; applicable rules; alternatives; recommendation; authority; unanswered action and deadline; expiry; expected reading minutes; consequence of delay | Briefing helper prepares; independent examiner approves readiness |
| Instruction and standing order | Exact answer; read-back; scope; reason; exceptions; effective/expiry dates; explicit authority or inferred preference; replaces/revokes; application and reversal history | Principal authors authority; helper proposes interpretation; examiner checks; clerk records |
| Result and examination | Immutable result version; each done line's check and evidence; environment; failures; unresolved assumptions; examiner identity; source observations; final acceptance or delegated closure | Maker deposits result; scripts and independent examiner add proof; clerk closes only when permitted |
| Return and delivery | Waiting-on owner/event; fallback date; no-answer consequence; delivery reference; attempts; durable receipt; next return; promise changes | Clerk creates and advances from admitted instructions |
| Trust and account | Work kind and context; matured acceptances; audits; misses; revoked authority; run costs; cycles and causes; attention events; restoration record | Clerk derives counts; examiner classifies defects; principal grants wider authority |

Never store credentials, sign-in codes, private reasoning, wholesale conversations or unnecessary customer data. Keep minimal redacted evidence and protected references to source material; the agreement names any needed retention limits. No helper's recollection is evidence. Removing sensitive material leaves a deletion receipt, not its contents.

An illustrative record for the test week's reminder-text goal, with proposed details rather than additional scenario facts:

| Moment | What actually sits in the register |
|---|---|
| Tuesday 12:40, received | Words: “The reminder texts go out too late, people miss their lesson”; proposed project: Driving app, unconfirmed; state: preparing; no sending authority; briefing due next sitting |
| Tuesday sitting, accepted | “Move the existing reminders earlier”; done: existing eligible bookings get the agreed 24-hour and 2-hour reminders, local time changes work, retries send no duplicates; exclude new audiences/channels; permit isolated changes and tests, retain existing sending consent; no live release without existing release authority; show Wednesday; cycle 1/par 2 |
| Wednesday, examined | Agreement version unchanged; result version 3; three done lines pass with replay evidence, clock-change cases and retry receipts; maker and examiner differ; live-release permission still required if absent; state: ready to show; next: accept or point at a defect; cycle remains 1 until he responds |

Every instruction gets a read-back: the receiver states intended action, boundary and return in its own words. An answer gets an immediate plain receipt; a material interpretive change becomes a fresh proposal, never authority smuggled into the receipt.

## 3. The runs

The chief of staff is a function exercised by brief writers and examiners, never a resident mind. Jobs start from the register, finish with a durable receipt, and exit; no chat transcript is handed on. A packet contains the accepted instructions, relevant current rules, changed facts and exact source references, usually 1–4 thousand words or less; larger evidence is fetched by reference with the reason and read volume recorded. Token allowances below count metered pieces of input/output text, not human reading time.

| Job and trigger | Reads → writes | Executor; lifetime; proposed effort allowance |
|---|---|---|
| Receive a wish or answer | Input and destination context → original words, receipt, next job | Script, seconds; local CPU only; speech transcription is a separately metered service |
| Prepare or revise a paper after intake, a fork or correction | Relevant agreement, examples, rules and sources → agreement, decision, sketch request or rule proposal | Helper, 1–5 minutes, roughly 2–5k input/output tokens; judgement required |
| Examine a paper before presentation or a new rule | Original instruction and sources, then draft → missing constraints, dissent, readiness verdict | Different helper, 1–3 minutes, 1–3k tokens; judgement required |
| Admit and dispatch after acceptance or a return | Current authority, dependencies, capacity and machine capabilities → reservations, work order, bounded packet | Script, seconds; matching ambiguous meaning goes to preparation, never guessed by script |
| Execute and read back after assignment | Packet plus necessary source files → read-back, checkpoint artifacts, proposed result or bounded wait | Helper, normally 5–30 minutes per slice, 6–10k tokens for a medium slice; judgement required |
| Test after a result or integration | Frozen result, check instructions and fixtures → criterion-level evidence | Scripts, seconds to minutes; CPU/storage metered; no judgement |
| Examine a result after script checks | Accepted purpose, result, independently obtained evidence → pass, defect or unresolved judgement | Different helper, 2–10 minutes, 4–8k tokens; judgement required |
| Commit an integrated result or undo an effect | Reservations, current base, approvals, checks and effect receipts → one committed version or restoration | Script, seconds plus checks; conflicts requiring judgement return to a doer |
| Prepare a showing after examination | Checked artifact and proof → direct result opening, assumptions, reserved human judgements | Helper, 1–2 minutes, 1–2k tokens; script rejects unsupported pass claims |
| Run gate, timers, delivery, reconciliation and accounts | New entries and due indexes → permitted transitions, returns, receipts, meter | Scripts, seconds, on events and each minute; never a helper polling |
| Reconcile rules/trust and compile weekly book | New rulings, audit results, counts → proposed rule changes, authority proposal, checked ledger | Helper only for interpretation, 2–5 minutes, 2–5k tokens shared across goals; clerk checks arithmetic |

A draft sketch is work and receives an independent examination before showing, although its taste remains his to judge. Rules, requests and ledgers with substantive interpretation receive paper examination too. Run limits include a cash ceiling; exceeding one returns a recorded reason and narrower next step, not another automatic conversation.

## 4. The gate

Precondition: a paper has current sources, examination, a named outcome for silence and a time. Unprepared papers return to staff; a script cannot reject protected dissent merely because the counterpart dislikes its conclusion. Only this gate may create an ordinary item for him.

1. **Standing order first.** Match explicit conditions, scope, exceptions and current authority. A valid order settles the case and records the application; inferred preference can support a recommendation but cannot grant permission. Ambiguous matches go to examination.
2. **Authorized default next.** If reversible action is within an accepted instruction's limits, schedule the recommendation for its stated time and proceed then without a question. A specifically reserved taste choice may occupy a sitting until its already-authorized fallback time: this is why Price page can appear in the morning yet use yearly at 5 pm. With no authority, the unanswered action is to park, preserve or restore, never to infer consent.
3. **Cap the principal's load.** Admit a briefing book of at most 12 estimated minutes, initially, ranked by consequence of delay, then age. Reserve two of those minutes for newly urgent needs; the ordinary budget is ten. Do not begin discretionary work unless its expected human review fits the next two sittings; existing commitments and remedial work take precedence. Excess goals get an explicit hold, reason and return date, visible in the glance, rather than hidden questions.
4. **Expire and revalidate.** Before placement or timeout action, withdraw anything whose goal, evidence, authority or choice changed. Every paper expires at its consequence deadline or within two working days, whichever is sooner; expiry may lead to re-preparation, not disappearance of a still-needed decision. Even held goals retain scheduled returns. At two working days of unresolved owner dependency, prepare one capacity tradeoff; never force an irreversible default to clear the queue.
5. **Next sitting.** Freeze a fresh book when he opens it, then revalidate each answer against current versions. Every item says the ask, recommendation, relevant uncertainty, unanswered action and exact time; direct evidence and dissent are one step away. Stop at the time budget, with the remaining commitments explicitly held. Corrections, “not now,” stop and revoke are actions on the same paper.
6. **Narrow urgent lane.** Bypass the sitting only for independently checked loss or harm before the next possible sitting that staff cannot avert under existing authority. Send one prepared, actionable alert through the same doorway's notification, request acknowledgement and record delivery; no status or ordinary completion qualifies. Coalesce related alerts, but never suppress a distinct genuine deadline to satisfy an interruption target. A checker may invoke this lane without counterpart permission.

An alert for the release says “Sign in before 3 pm or release waits a week,” opens the actual sign-in, and returns at 2:30 if not acknowledged, assuming these notices still precede the deadline. The clerk checks completed sign-in independently; tapping a notification is not completion. If there is no answer by 3, record the missed window and park the release. No password goes through the office.

This is a time and age budget, not a magical seven-paper limit. Estimate error is metered; sustained overload lowers admissions, never hides bad news. At five hundred matters a day, clerks may settle hundreds under narrow orders; if fifty genuinely require him, this office cannot preserve both throughput and silence and must obtain a priority or scope change.

## 5. The checks

Before acceptance, the briefing helper pairs each plain done line with an observable example, counterexample, proof source, checking actor and freshness boundary. “Faster sign-up” needs an agreed baseline, relevant users and measurable improvement; it cannot quietly become “the patch compiles.” If a line cannot yet be checked, propose the cheapest experiment or sketch and say what remains uncertain in the agreement.

Scripts check things with determinate answers: input constraints, tests against agreed examples, duplicates, permissions, unchanged required data, artifact identity and whether all required evidence exists. A script run records command/check version, environment, input and output references, result version and observation time. Tests that merely repeat the implementation do not establish the user's outcome.

The second helper first reads the accepted purpose and criteria, then inspects the actual result without the maker's verdict. It must independently exercise a relevant path, try a counterexample and identify one assumption or say why none remains; the register rejects a bare “looks good.” It may demand missing proof and cannot repair the result it is checking. Its disagreement is preserved verbatim with source access and cannot be edited by a brief writer.

The checker is a different run and identity with no shared conversation; use a different implementation or model family where practical for consequential judgement. This reduces shared bias, not guarantees independence. Periodic known-defect rehearsals and random re-examination measure whether checking catches anything; a checker that misses them loses eligibility. Safety-critical or legally consequential work needs the appropriate qualified checker, not two general helpers agreeing.

Only he judges his reserved taste, priorities or willingness to take a newly discovered risk. He sees the artifact at the relevant location, “6 of 6 checked,” assumptions and unresolved judgements; each opens its actual evidence. Proof is tied to the exact result version, so later changes invalidate affected checks. Progress is passed done lines, never confidence prose; acceptance and observed correctness are separate entries.

## 6. The watch

A scheduled clerk on an always-on host reads due times every minute. A second host independently watches that clerk and the record; helpers need not remain awake. All deadlines use the register's clock, with the principal's local time displayed; sleep and reconnect cause an overdue sweep before new work starts. The following intervals are initial operating limits, not findings from research.

| Clock | Trip and scripted response |
|---|---|
| Intake and queue | No durable receipt in 30 seconds: sender retains and retries the same request; no pickup in two minutes: try another eligible machine; after two attempts hold and forecast impact |
| Worker ownership | Five-minute permission to submit, renewed each minute by the local clerk only for an observed live process within its checkpoint budget; expiry cancels permission for late submissions before a new attempt is admitted, without claiming the old process died |
| Useful progress and run budget | No promised artifact/checkpoint within 15 minutes, or declared slice/cash limit exceeded: inspect actual process/effects, isolate or stop where possible, then retry once or prepare a recovery decision |
| Dependency and bring-forward | Every external wait has an event and a fallback date, normally within one working day; event delivery or due time reopens the goal, checks relevance and applies authorized fallback or prepares a decision |
| Shared resource | Reservation ends with submission permission; a stale holder cannot commit; time-limited effect credentials expire too, and an uncertain external effect is reconciled before another attempt |
| Promised showing | Forecast on every material change and at least hourly; if the promise cannot hold, record one revised promise and one reason for the next opening; imminent material loss uses the urgent lane |
| Machine reachability | Two missed one-minute probes mark it unavailable; reroute portable work; machine-bound work stays held, never assumed complete; assess affected promises immediately |
| Questions, defaults and holds | At every return/deadline: revalidate scope, evidence and authority; execute the authorized default or park; invalidate stale papers and reprepare necessary ones; two-working-day age guard runs even if he never sits |
| Notice delivery | No durable delivery receipt within one minute: resend the same notice; urgent acknowledgement missing at its stated return: one reminder or an approved alternate channel, before the last useful action time |
| Observation and observer | Publish last successful scan each minute; second host detects three-minute staleness within the next minute, marks state unknown and attempts takeover; it also probes the record, rather than trusting the first clerk's heartbeat |
| Rules and trust | Check expiry at every use; material context change freezes affected authority immediately; daily scan finds due renewals and matured outcomes; weekly audit schedule cannot be skipped because throughput is high |
| Accounts and retention | Weekly ledger closes from event times; missing prices stay unknown; retention expiries delete only material eligible under the agreement, with receipts; unresolved proof is retained within that authority |

Unknown never appears as “all well”: the doorway shows observation age and “not observed since …”. The independent observer can send one service-failure notice through a separately configured route if the doorway is unavailable. If both observers or all communication paths fail, timely warning is impossible; the screen can still reveal its own stale timestamp on reopening, not promise continuous supervision.

## 7. Rules and trust

Every answer creates a ruling immediately, with its source and scope; not every ruling becomes a universal order. The same receipt proposes the narrow interpretation. For the email correction, ask “this email, or future welcome emails too?” once, on the artifact; absent an answer, apply only here. Explicit “always” becomes standing authority only within the named scope, reason, exceptions, limits and review date. An inferred preference is separately labelled advice and never licenses an effect.

Before drafting or dispatch, the clerk retrieves current rules by agreed project, work kind, consequence and conditions, with their source cases. Helpers may search for less obvious relevant cases, but a claimed match must cite its conditions; contradictory or uncertain matches require examination. Precedence is: current explicit revocation or case instruction, applicable explicit narrow order, explicit wider order, then advisory preference. Equal-authority conflicts freeze only the disputed effect and produce one prepared choice; recency alone does not justify overriding a different scope.

A reversal marks the source rule disputed, stops new applications, lists affected work and opens bounded re-examination; it does not silently rewrite history. Changed law, audience, repository, check method or risk invalidates applicability. Initial review dates are 30 days for preferences and the agreement's end for delegated effects; unchanged facts may support an examined renewal inside already-given authority, never an automatic expansion.

Trust belongs to a kind of work plus its context and check method, not to a helper. Count first-time acceptance separately from outcomes that remain correct through the agreed observation window, initially seven days. Also count send-backs, escaped defects, sampled passes, rework and check failures; do not improve scores by relabelling failures as a different kind.

Three first-time wording acceptances trigger the scenario's proposal to stop showing routine wording, but do not themselves grant it. Require three matured relevant outcomes, functioning checks, successful random audits and explicit standing permission before par can move from two to one or zero. Where the window has not elapsed, the proposal says so and holds the wider authority. At zero, every result still has independent checking, one in five is randomly re-examined, and the weekly book links all closures. These are pilot thresholds, not statistical proof.

Trust removes human showings and possibly repeat agreements within a standing order; it never removes independent proof, budget limits or a reserved judgement. Irreversible or newly consequential acts keep explicit approval regardless of score. An escaped defect immediately freezes the affected shortcut, restores full showing, rechecks work since the last sound audit and narrows or revokes the wrong rule; recovery follows recorded authority, with a new question only if a new decision is needed.

Stop, amend, revoke and restore are first-class instructions: fence outstanding submissions, cancel uncommitted effects, find affected results and preserve evidence. Already-completed external effects require their own authorized remedy; a stop receipt states what has and has not actually been stopped. The principal and a checker can open original submissions and an independent examination without the chief-of-staff role's permission.

## 8. The meter

The register writes these alongside ordinary work: wish received; agreement version and par; every presentation and response; reason for a new cycle; timers and waiting intervals; authority/rule applications; checked closure; later defects; each run's input, cached input, output, tool charges, runtime and evidence read. Runs, retries and failed work all charge the same goal; shared weekly work is shown separately and allocated by an explicit stable rule, initially equally over active goals.

A cycle is one request for his response on a goal, ending with that response or its stated timeout; a passive notification and an unviewed paper are not cycles but still count as attention costs if pushed. Record raw cycles, par fixed at acceptance, and causes: preventable misunderstanding, useful discovery, changed desire, external change, necessary escalation, correction. Revised par remains a new visible version; never erase an overrun or call every extra cycle waste.

The doorway measures active reading/answering time, words rendered and typed, taps/keys, unique required destinations, voluntary sittings and pushed interruptions; pause on loss of focus or 30 seconds idle. Sign-in or other outside action gets an elapsed-time proxy clearly labelled, optionally corrected by him. Unobserved reading, actual mental load and voice thinking time remain unknown, not zero; an optional weekly “anything you kept remembering?” check tests the offloading claim.

Per goal: cycles against original/current par; his measured and estimated minutes; waiting age; quality outcomes; cost = charged tokens at actual rates + tool/compute charges + allocated overhead. Missing charges show coverage and an unknown remainder. Re-read ratio comes from repeated source versions/bytes and repeated packet tokens, not an invented estimate of model attention. All measures derive from receipts; no separate reporting task for him.

## 9. The fourteen events

1. **Morning sitting:** clerk revalidates a book of Driver ratings agreement, Invoice export artifact with six passes/two assumptions, and Price page choice; roughly eight minutes fits the ordinary ten. Answers and read-backs are recorded; the book then says nothing needs him, with observation time.
2. **12:40 voice:** intake clerk receipts the words, briefing helper proposes the Driving app context and a short agreement, examiner checks it; no project form. Only the examined read-back reaches the next sitting; costly work waits for acceptance unless an existing order covers it.
3. **Seventeen questions:** rule lookup settles eleven and records their sources; three reversible recommendations use authorized deadlines. Staff prepare the other three owner choices, with no-answer actions and due times, for the available minutes; overflow holds the affected goals explicitly.
4. **Wrong welcome email:** his pointing creates a located defect and the single here/always choice on the same artifact; receipt records the ruling. A doer revises and a different examiner checks; only the corrected sketch returns, with a useful correction cycle recorded.
5. **Not now:** clerk parks Driver ratings, releases reservations and records “return when Faster sign-up closes by acceptance or delegated authority,” plus a next-working-day fallback check. The dependency event reopens readiness; he need not remember it or receive daily reminders.
6. **Silent forty minutes:** ownership/progress clocks trip within six/sixteen minutes respectively; clerk fences the attempt, reconciles effects and reroutes once. Receipts and new proof enter the register; if the promised showing holds, nothing reaches him.
7. **Computer off overnight:** reachability clerk marks it unavailable within three minutes and discovers machine-bound work cannot keep Thursday. It records Friday and shows once, at his next opening, “Shows Friday: the computer holding the required environment was off”; time-critical consequences would use the urgent lane.
8. **Release sign-in:** examined alert opens the actual sign-in before its last useful time, with the 3 pm deadline and week-long slip; delivery/acknowledgement clocks run. Completion is independently observed, or the clerk parks the release at expiry; this is a required interruption.
9. **Dead question:** revalidation links the changed work, withdraws the old paper and invalidates late answers. A one-line notice in the next book/ledger says what was withdrawn; no answer is requested.
10. **Three wording acceptances:** account clerk records the streak; staff prepare the reduced-showing proposal with maturity and audit evidence. He sees the proposal once, including unmet conditions; wider authority waits until those conditions and his permission exist.
11. **Accepted result later wrong:** defect receipt freezes affected trust/rules, starts re-examination and authorized repair/restore, and preserves the original acceptance. One plain notice states the defect and remedy; only a consequential choice staff cannot make becomes a question, urgent if delay harms.
12. **Three days away:** on return, reconcile first; show missed promises, current observation age and any uncontained harm before ordinary papers, then a fresh finite book. Budget two minutes to orient and at most twelve for the sitting; excess owner work is explicitly held, so full resolution has no honest fixed-time guarantee.
13. **Weekly ledger:** clerk derives goals done, cycles/par and causes, minutes with coverage, delegated choices, learned rules, send-backs/reasons and this week's versus last week's questions. An examined short ledger opens voluntarily; all closed artifacts and defect history remain reachable.
14. **Curiosity:** one level down opens observed assignments by computer, last evidence/age, costs and charge coverage for the chosen goal. Scripts build this from receipts; no helper starts and nothing is pushed.

Replay accounting: one required doorway; one required urgent interruption in event 8; promise/withdrawal/defect notices normally wait for opening, with additional pushes only if their actual consequence qualifies. The initial sitting supplies eight minutes; the scenario omits enough later answers, text and durations that an exact weekly total would be fabricated. Replay must retain those unknowns, count every displayed word/tap, and test whether held goals, observation failure and return notices create memory burdens in use.

## 10. What breaks it

| Failure | Catch, deadline and recovery |
|---|---|
| Helper stops silently | Missing ownership renewal within six minutes or useful progress within sixteen; fence outputs, inspect effects, bounded retry; no promise change means no human interruption |
| Helper waits for something that never arrives | A wait without owner, event and fallback is rejected immediately; due clerk rechecks by its explicit return, initially no later than next working day, then falls back inside authority or parks/prepares a choice |
| Message lost | Sender retries the same reference after 30 seconds without a durable receipt; due scan rediscovers unclaimed orders within two minutes; committed delivery register and one-minute reconciliation recover lost completions without double effects |
| False “done” | Maker cannot change checked state; scripts and examiner block closure before showing; concealed defects may escape, so random audits and later observations reopen them, with no guaranteed discovery time for unobservable defects |
| Restart and forgetting | New run receives current bounded packet and read-back requirement before any effect; stale attempt cannot submit; pending decisions and effects are in the register, not its memory |
| Two helpers change the same thing | Reserve shared resources before dispatch; private working copies; one admitted version can commit against a given base; conflicting or changed bases trigger integration and fresh checks before showing |
| Computer off | Two missed probes, caught within three minutes; portable jobs reroute; local-only inputs wait for return, and showing promises are revised once if necessary |
| Record unreachable | Clerks notice on their next minute; stop committing work and external effects that cannot verify authority, keep local receipts/results for later reconciliation, display unknown and use independent service alert; restore from tested copies before reopening admission |
| Wrong rule | Boundary checks and source comparison before use; dissent/defect/audit disputes it, blocks further matching immediately, and traces affected applications; a semantically wrong rule can remain undetected until contrary evidence arrives |
| No answers for five days | Authorized defaults run, expired questions revalidate, age guard holds new owner-dependent work after two working days; promises honestly slip and urgent deadlines take their stated no-answer outcome; return opens a prepared finite book, never five days of chatter |

An expired assignment fences submission, not a running process or an already-issued transfer. External effects must pass an authority-checking clerk using one-use operation references and expiring permissions; if a target cannot prevent/reconcile duplicates, that operation cannot be automatically retried. During total record loss, unsafe work stops: no private shadow register is promoted merely because it is reachable. The register permits one active writer generation; takeover must invalidate the old writer before granting new effect permissions. Backup restoration must reconcile effect receipts before allowing further effects.

## 11. Cost and where the office ends

A medium, unfamiliar goal with two work slices, no taste sketch and no fork uses six helper runs: agreement writer, paper examiner, two doers, result examiner and showing writer. It also uses roughly 15–30 event-driven clerk invocations for intake/admission, tests, commit and accounts, plus 120 tiny watch invocations per hour across two hosts, shared across all goals rather than charged to each; measure their actual CPU/storage cost. Allow 20–40k helper tokens as a planning range, with a 60k goal ceiling before re-planning; price with actual rates, not a universal dollar estimate. A new generalized rule adds preparation and examination; a sketch adds maker/checker runs and its planned human cycle.

For illustration, 30k charged tokens at an effective measured rate of P dollars per million cost 0.03P dollars before tools and compute; separate cached/uncached/output rates are used in the real ledger. Count the examiner and show-preparer reading again as overhead. Human allowance is about one minute for agreement plus a three-minute showing, not a measured guarantee.

Against today's resident coordinator plus workers, the design spends more on early paper examination, independent observation, structured evidence and rule upkeep. It spends less on repeated full-history reading, idle polling, retelling status and pairwise negotiation: each change wakes one bounded job, and workers coordinate through reservations/results. The method reports roughly fourteen coordinator turns per round and 68% rereading; those are corpus symptoms, not a prediction that six jobs will cost less or be faster. Compare replay and live cost per correctly completed goal, including recovery and setup; identical underlying work remains work.

I leave the historical centre where paper movement becomes the job: no serial deputy hierarchy, no clerk re-summarizing a clerk, and no mandatory human briefing for a matter already settled by authority. Parallel doers and a shared event register replace the chief's personal memory; deterministic admission replaces office negotiation. At five hundred matters, inspect exception rate, preparation cost and age: if genuine owner decisions exceed available minutes, priority/scope must change. No office procedure can manufacture the principal's judgement or unlimited review capacity.

## 12. Taking part from anywhere

Participation needs a small contract, not a shared assistant brand: authenticate an identity and capabilities; obtain one admitted order and bounded sources; acknowledge/read it back; work privately within permission; deposit an immutable result or an explicit wait; report usage and exit. A local wrapper may do receipt, renewal and reporting for a helper that only reads text and writes files. That helper cannot claim it has run a check it cannot execute; capability matching supplies a separate checker. Offline work is limited to already-authorized private preparation, with no commit after permission expires.

The contract must expose receive, claim with expiry, inspect current authority, append result/proof, declare wait, and reconcile receipt. Reserve dependencies and shared resources before parallel work starts. Task boards can exchange work orders/results through a wrapper; automation services can be clerks or run checks. Neither board state nor “job succeeded” counts as independent proof of the requested outcome. Existing candidates are mapped only in the final section, after the design.

## 13. The smallest first version

Start with one private register, immutable artifact storage, bounded order packets, one dispatcher and independent minute watch, two kinds of helper, and a single phone/desk doorway with say/sit/glance. Include agreements, one examined artifact showing, explicit authority/defaults, dependency returns, exclusive commits, stop/revoke/restore receipts and the full meter. Use only one low-risk reversible work kind; support a second computer and simulate its disappearance before admitting cross-machine effects. Keep all results independently checked and shown initially.

Leave out generalized preference inference, automatic trust promotion, voice, elaborate staffing, broad resource optimization and historical imports. A manually authored standing order is sufficient to avoid a repeated question; a durable timer is sufficient to remove a check-in. Implement the thin contract and screen, not replacement worker tools; plan ten to fifteen focused engineering days including fault injection, permission boundaries and first-goal instrumentation, a judgement estimate rather than a commitment. Expand only after replaying this week and completing real goals with receipts.

The first comparison fixes goal boundaries and done lines, then measures preventable cycles, owner minutes, setup/recovery effort and cost against the recorded current pattern, with escaped defects as a guardrail. It should beat today by eliminating at least one repeated question/check-in on a real goal without reducing checking; par two is a target, not evidence. A failure to improve after accounting for office overhead rejects this version rather than justifying more procedure.

## 14. Three weakest points and the method

1. **Prepared papers can still hide the decisive fact.** Source access, protected dissent and random re-examination make filtering contestable, but cannot prove that omitted unknowns do not exist. Brief quality remains the largest judgement dependency.
2. **The office may cost more than it saves.** Six bounded helpers plus receipts can exceed one competent short coordinator on simple work; high-volume ambiguous cases make classification and examination the bottleneck. Measure it and collapse unnecessary paper preparation before adding another layer.
3. **Checking and trust share blind spots.** Different runs can share training errors; three matured outcomes and one-in-five audits are deliberately conservative pilot rules, not calibrated reliability. Generalize authority slowly and never let trust waive approval of an irreversible consequence.

The method's absolutes need changing: attention is scarce alongside money, correctness and recovery capacity; a minute can confirm a bounded interpretation but not guarantee settled intent. “Decide once” means preserve scope and revisit on contrary facts, not freeze preferences; silence cannot create consent. Extra cycles can be discovery or useful bad news, and final acceptance is not a correctness measurement. Two-cycle par is a hypothesis; zero cycles still incurs audit, failure and eventual human recovery costs. Evidence basis: the method, plan §§2–3, shared scenario, and research/d5-outside.md §§1–4, especially authority, observer failure, sampling, dependency coordination and private-office records; no prior design was used.

| Windmill | Scripts, flows, schedules and inspectable jobs are candidates for clerks, timed returns and determinate checks on an always-on host | Office rules, receipt semantics and permission checks are application work; independence requires another observer outside that host/service |

None of these alone supplies the accepted agreement, version-bound independent proof, scoped authority, calibrated trust, observer-of-observer, cross-machine effect protection and finite principal briefing as one verified system. Start by joining their smallest usable interfaces to the one register; replace a candidate only when that contract cannot be met.
