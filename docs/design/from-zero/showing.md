## Only you can judge

Three decisions. Nothing is built until you give the second one.

**1. Which feel?** Two designers built the same structure from different starting points. Both are finished enough to click.

- *Sitting-based* (`v2-sitting`): compact and structured. The leaner of the two.
- *Letter-based* (`v2-letter`): warmer, reads like a letter, with a worked example in each say-back. About a quarter more reading.

I would build the sitting-based one and borrow the letter's warmth in its wording. This is taste, so it is yours. It can be changed later at the cost of a restyle.

**2. Go on stage 1, and where?** Stage 1 is the check before showing, with the meter: two to four days of building to first real use, on private work that can be undone. I would run it first on the building of this system itself, in the agent-annotate repository, which is where you chose to try the thin coordinator earlier. Each later stage is then built through the earlier one. Second, the commerce project. Third, the vehicle project, where your load is highest and the risk of disturbing live work is highest too. This waits for your go because building is not cheap to undo.

**3. Helpers and computers one level down?** On 12 Nov you asked to see workers per computer on the front page. Both designs put every goal of every project on one page, and put "who is working where" one tap below it. I would keep it there: a front page that shows the engine room invites checking. Your call. Easy to change.

## What the evidence overturned

**The agreement does not save cycles.** I called it "the most valuable minute". Two judges, working apart, replayed 36 of your past goals. An agreement written blind from your first sentence would have prevented about 2 of 416 returns, and as a sitting of its own it adds one per goal. About half of all returns were decisions and discoveries that only existed once there was work to look at.

So the method changed:

- **The say-back no longer waits for you.** It arrives as the work starts and you can fix a line at any time. It waits for your go only when the work is costly, risky or cannot be undone.
- **The centre is showing something cheap and checked, early**, with your decisions gathered where you can see what they are about.
- **A result with nothing left for you to judge is told, not asked**, with See and Undo. One in a few is still shown, so the checks stay honest.
- **The check comes first.** It is the largest lever in your own messages, ahead of everything else.

**An honest expectation.** With every part working, the judges' best case is a little over half of today's returns. The rest are your own decisions and discoveries. The method can gather them and make each one cheaper. It cannot remove them, and it does not promise par on every goal.

## What would be built

One record carries your sentence through every showing, correction and acceptance, so cycles can be counted. Short-lived helpers each do one job from an exact packet and exit. A maker can never pass its own work. A clock, not a helper, watches for stalls, and something watches the clock. The record lives on M1 Max.

| Stage | Built | You can then |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The check before showing; the meter; a say-back that does not wait | Open a result that someone other than its maker has exercised, and point at a fault |
| 2 | The watch and the glance | Leave, and come back to dated truth |
| 3 | One record across every helper; one doorway for all projects; defaults | Say a thing once; change helpers without explaining again |
| 4 | Rules and trust | Decide a recurring case once; stop being shown routine work |

Stage 1 must earn stage 2: six matched pairs of real goals, alternating today's way and the checked way. The checked way must cut at least one return per goal and your minutes, with no new escaped fault. If it does not, it is narrowed or dropped.

## What is not proved

- Nothing has been run. Every design is a drawing and every figure for the new method is a judgement about the past.
- The record has no measure of your minutes, and most goals have no recorded acceptance.
- No phone has been used. The lock-screen glance, the one interruption and "try it on your phone" all depend on a phone path that is untested.
- The replay tested one way of writing an agreement. It does not prove no way could do better.
