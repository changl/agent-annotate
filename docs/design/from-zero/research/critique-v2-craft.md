# Critique of round 2: craft and first impression (critic "craft")

I rendered all four prototypes in headless Chrome at 390 px (inside an iframe) and at 1280 px, in light and dark. I walked every v2-sitting moment step by step through its own path (110 shots), opened 32 v2-letter states, 13 showing kinds and 22 return states, and ran every `#check`; all pass. Every prototype was opened before its notes. Files were current as of 02:30. States are named by each file's own moment list.

## 1. Cold open: the first ten seconds

**v2-sitting, phone.**
- *Lock card:* "Seen 2 min ago · 1 thing · about 3 minutes · Gathered for your 8:30", with "Say it" below. Understood at once: one thing, three minutes, fresh.
- *Opened:* a large serif count, two told lines, LOOK · Invoice export, the check line, YOURS TO JUDGE, the file, and "Yes, this file" at the thumb. The count, then the file, are the most visible things. That is right.
- *Not understood:* the black dots with "6 proved ›", and "made 07:02 · 07:12" (two unlabelled times).

**v2-sitting, desk.** The door card is on the left. On the right, unasked, is "Where everything stands": project headings with counts, a status column ("sketch Wed", "with you", "done Mon") and a blue Stop link each. **His first sight on the desk is a project status board.** After Open, the facing page is a fixed card about 520 px tall, with the thing small in its top-left, while the left sheet scrolls and is cramped.

**v2-letter, phone.**
- *Lock card:* "Your 8:30 sitting · 1 thing · about 3 minutes", with "Hold, and say what you want".
- *Opened:* "Good morning. One thing, about 3 minutes.", then "TO KNOW. NOTHING TO DO." and the question to judge. The file sits behind a "Look ›" card at the foot of the first screen. It is one tap away, then full screen with its own answer button. That trade is acceptable.
- *Not understood:* why this card shows no "seen" age when the quiet card does.

**v2-letter, desk.** A real book spread: the letter on the left, the file at full width on the right, already open, with "Give it the whole desk". Round 1's grey skeleton is gone. **This is the best first desk of any round.**

**v2-showing, desk.** A clean review screen: check line, the thing, and a right column with what to judge, what was proved and two buttons. A dashed grey "THE HOST · letter or sitting" placeholder takes the left 230 px in every state. He will read it as unfinished.

**v2-return.** The lock card ends "Ten now covers what costs most", which is cryptic. Opened on the desk it is two pages, "what is so" and "what needs you, cut to ten minutes": the clearest return drawn.

## 2. Does either look like a tool he already has?

**v2-sitting has lost the lesson-app feel on the phone:** no Begin, no segmented progress, no "2 of 4 · 6 min left", no ✕. What is still tool-like:
- "Looks right. Undo" pop-up notices, like a mail app's.
- Dots with "4 proved ›", like the pass marks of an automatic code check.
- A numbered queue of later items ("3 · DECIDE · Gift cards · 2 min"), like a task list.
- Build numbers inside the buttons, like a developer tool; and a desk door that is a project dashboard.

**v2-letter kept the letter:** a dated header and greeting, "To know. Nothing to do.", receipts in blue italic ink ("Yes. It goes to the accountant on the 1st of each month. Stop it any time, from its page."), "That is all.", and the desk spread. It imported three tool parts: the "Use it / Mark it" switch, step circles with "3 s" bubbles, and lavender "Only you can judge" boxes that read as form sections. **It is still the only design that does not read as work software.**

**The studies.** v2-showing looks like a code-review approval panel: check line, numbered proof list, and "Looks right · build 214" where Approve would be. Inside the letter's page that matters less. v2-return reads like a calm report.

- **Phone, Where everything stands:** "14 · Stop", and "14 more, on their dates ›" with nothing listed above.

**v2-letter**
- **"sitting", a method word, on his most-seen surfaces:** "Your 8:30 sitting" (lock card), "8:30 SITTING" (sheet header), "your sittings are 10" (return), "the 100 your sittings allow" and "outside a sitting" (week), "Is two sittings a week your pace now?" (4 Dec).
- **Also:** "a first slice" (return list); "3 s · 2 s · 1 s" bubbles and blue dots on the step circles (Thu 26 Nov journey); "Hold it" beside "See the corrected file", which reads like press-and-hold (Thu 19 Nov). "Give it the whole desk" is plain enough; keep it.

**The studies**
- **v2-showing ids:** "fingerprint 7f3a 91c0" (I), "preview b41" (B), "billing b40, then b41" (J). It also uses "Checked by someone else" and "Proved to work · by someone else": two verbs for one idea.
- **v2-return:** "Ten now covers what costs most." (1·1); "10 need you. Nine things.", where the numbers disagree (1·2).

## 4. The showing sheet

- **Reads at a glance:** "Checked by someone else" and "Yours to judge", the two best labels of the round.
- **Proof is said twice.** On the desk, "Proved to work · by someone else" repeats the check line, and "Not proved: old phones" comes back one line lower as "Your yes does not cover: old phones". Say it once, with one verb.
- **The caveat comes after the decision.** "Your yes does not cover" sits under the buttons in v2-showing at both sizes. v2-sitting already puts it above, in amber, on the phone.
- **v2-sitting turns proof into a code** of unlabelled dots and "N proved ›". v2-letter's line is better: "Checked by someone who did not make it: 6 of 6 'done' lines pass", plus "Not tried: …" only when there is one, so a safeguard with nothing to report takes no words.
- **Verdict:** on the desk it is a short form (about 70 to 190 words per kind, by its own check). On the phone it reads at a glance once the caveat moves up.
- **Pointing:** obvious in v2-showing (Mark freezes the frame, a red "Tap what's wrong" tag, a dashed outline on everything markable); clear in v2-letter ("Use it / Mark it", "Touch what is wrong"). **Not obvious in v2-sitting**, whose only sign is a 12 px grey "touch what's wrong" in the corner of the header.

## 5. Back after five days

- **v2-sitting, Thu 26 Nov.** Card: "Welcome back · 2 things now · About 4 minutes; 2 more at 5 pm." Opened: "Five days. 36 of your 40 goals need nothing from you.", a bar that adds up to 40, and five labelled lines (Went wrong, Couldn't see, Decided for you, Running, Faster). That is about 110 words, current in about 30 seconds. **He does come away knowing what changed.** Two limits: "9 changed" cannot be matched to the lines shown, and it is the easy case ("Went wrong: nothing").
- **v2-letter, Thu 26 Nov.** About 210 words come before the first item (What the wait cost, Went ahead inside what you allowed with Undo on each, Held, Which six): current in about a minute. The whole sitting is 799 words, against v2-sitting's 459. Honest, but heavy.
- **v2-return, 1·1 to 1·3.** Draws the hard case (a miss, a lost week, ten goals waiting) on two desk pages, with "Start: 10 minutes".
- **Merged into neither whole design:** that two-page desk, and a return where something did go wrong.

## 6. Defects in the screenshots

**v2-sitting**
- **Pop-up notices cover the page (phone 390).** They float mid-screen for about a second after each answer:
  - Wed 08:30: over "That is all for now. Nothing else needs you."
  - Thu 17:00: "Released 4.11 to 1 in 10 at 17:04. Stop the rest" runs off the right edge and covers the trust offer's buttons.
  - Thu 26 Nov: over the Sales tax "done means" lines he must read before Go.
  - Fri 08:30: over the price page itself.
- **Crushed buttons (Fri 08:30):** "Looks right · page as served at 07:55" wraps to three lines in a 175 px pill (phone); "Looks right · build 4.12 on an iPhone 13" puts "Looks right" on two lines (desk and phone).
- **Desk overlaps (1280):** the Mark button overflows the sheet card (Fri 08:30, Price page); the fixed "Looks right" bar covers "3 proved ›" (Thu 26 Nov).
- **Tue 17:00:** on the phone the first-slice screens are about 100 px wide with clipped labels ("Phone (optior", "waits for your"); nobody can judge a screen at that size. On the desk they sit small in an empty facing page, while the two choices about them sit in the left column.
- **Dark mode:** the lock cards stay light cream on the dark lock screen (Tue 08:30, Wed 13:10, Wed 15:40), which is glare at night. v2-letter's dark card is right.

**v2-letter**
- "Walk through it first" and "See both widths first" are main buttons drawn disabled, white on pale lavender, about 2:1 contrast (Thu 26 Nov, phone, both themes).
- The left page cuts off its last line, "It goes to nobody until you say" (Tue 08:30, 1280).

**v2-showing**
- The "THE HOST" skeleton column (desk) and the "host · ‹ back to the sitting" bar (phone) appear in every state.
- **Bad wraps at 390:** "ABOUT 30 / S" (F); "SECOND VERSION · ABOUT 1 MIN" (A, second version); "Looks right" on two lines between Mark and "Switch on for real…" (H).
- **Collision:** the "Tap what's wrong." tag sits on the Phone width switch (B, mark mode, phone).

## 7. Best and worst screens of round 2

**Best: v2-showing I, the release, at the pause (phone).** The sentence "Release Driving app 2.4 (214) to 1 in 10 iPhone users today at 14:00, and to everyone by Monday" is set large in serif behind a red rule. Below it: two choices, one marked "I'd say this: it can be stopped until Monday"; "Still not proved: iPhone 8 and older"; then "Correct. Release it." to press and hold, beside "Not yet". Calm and exact before a step that cannot be undone. Runner-up: v2-return's back-after-five-days desk spread.

**Worst: v2-sitting Fri 20 Nov 08:30, the price page at two widths (phone).** It is meant to answer "things that move", but shows a three-line crushed "Looks right · page as served at 07:55", a notice floating over the page, and only the phone width without scrolling. Runner-up: v2-showing's desk with its placeholder column, which repeats round 1's fault.

## 9. Recommendation

**Main design: v2-letter.** It is the only one that still does not look like work software. Its desk is designed, and its proof takes no words when there is nothing to report. It makes him walk a journey and see both widths before he can accept, and his choices sit on the screens they change.

**Graft three things from v2-sitting first:** the five-line return summary over the bar that adds up to 40 (half the letter's return words); "Away, by the look of it", the card shown when he is away without having said so; and the scrubbable recording with named chapters ("Name and phone 0:00 · Email 0:14 …"). If v2-sitting stays the main design, items 1, 5, 6, 7 and 8 are the minimum before he sees it.

**The difference is now mostly feel, plus those mechanics.** By their own counters, on the phone, v2-sitting is 3,092 words, 40 taps and 22 decisions; v2-letter is 3,067 words, 49 taps and 20 decisions.

**Do not show both as rivals.** Show one whole design end to end, then near the end a 60-second side-by-side of the same moment in both (Tue 08:30, phone). That is two concrete alternatives, as revision 3 asks.

**Say aloud what is assumed:** recordings on his phone, "Try it on your phone" opening the exact build, and a lock card that ages by itself. None was proved on a real phone.
