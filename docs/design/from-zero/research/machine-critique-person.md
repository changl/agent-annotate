# MC3 — What the person lives through

References below: R = [record-first](../machine/record-first.md), C = [check-first](../machine/check-first.md), O = [office-first](../machine/office-first.md), P = [capsule-first](../machine/capsule-first.md); section numbers refer to those files. This is an independent reading through his attention, not a running-system test. No other critic's work was consulted.

## Where the four agree

- One work doorway on phone and desk; workers, machines and raw proof are optional depth. A helper's memory never owns his obligations.
- Original intent, accepted scope, authority, exact result and independent checks survive every restart. “Reported”, “observed”, “checked” and “accepted” differ.
- Prepare the reaction before asking; settle covered choices internally. Reversibility alone grants no authority, and timeout never accepts a new goal.
- Reserve human minutes before starting work; expire stale questions, park real obligations with working return plans, and catch stalls without asking him.
- A correction first settles its own case. Broader authority is explicit; three acceptances alone prove neither correctness nor permanent taste.
- Quiet has an observation age and an honest unknown state; defects reopen history, reduce freedom and trigger checked repair. Count recovery and policy work too.

This convergence is strong agreement about responsibilities, not four independent demonstrations of benefit: the authors shared the method and outside evidence. Their ordinary human journeys are almost the same. Most differences in records and job names buy him nothing unless they change a failed day.
