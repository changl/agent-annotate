## Fields, in the requested order

### 1. Chief of staff / private office
Procedure: officials prepare a submission, private office sifts it, and the principal receives a decision package. The DWP template puts issue, recommendation and timing on its first page; the issue has a word limit, each recommendation is separate, urgency needs a reason, and clearance precedes submission. Briefing books also contain an agenda, meeting objective, participant details and background. [DWP template, pp. 2–8](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7f4e1be5274a2e87db50bc/foi-248-2011-ministerial-sub.pdf)
Attention/protection: principal decides once per submission; staff do the chasing and triage. DWP targets a reading time below five minutes, not a measured decision time. IfG describes delegated submissions and separating immediate decisions, long-term priorities and overdue decisions; this is practice evidence. [IfG](https://www.instituteforgovernment.org.uk/publication/submission-work-special-adviser)
Failure/carry-over: a private office can fill the diary and bury the principal in papers; agree priorities, format and briefing frequency explicitly. Copy the prepared decision and staff-owned chasing; do not assume silence grants authority. **Untraced:** a professional private-office bring-forward-file template or a general “unless otherwise directed” consent rule. [IfG working guide](https://www.instituteforgovernment.org.uk/publication/private-office)

### 2. Military orders
Procedure: the five-paragraph order covers situation, mission, execution, sustainment, and command/signal. The immediate **confirmation brief** checks what the subordinate understood; the later **backbrief** checks the subordinate's proposed execution. A map rehearsal walks interactions and contingencies, with changes recorded in the order. These are different gates, not synonyms. [Army order format](https://rdl.train.army.mil/catalog-ws/view/100.ATSC%2FB10ACFFB-EF0F-4403-8E55-F291E3D899A1-1615388530254/tc2_19x01.pdf), [CALL handbook, ch. 5](https://api.army.mil/e2/c/downloads/2023/01/19/48e6a637/19-18-commander-and-staff-guide-to-rehearsals-a-no-fail-approach-handbook-jul-19-public.pdf)
Attention/protection: commander gives intent, hears confirmation, and selectively hears plans/rehearsals. One handbook technique limits the confirmation session to 15 minutes and individual backbriefs to 10 minutes; these are limits, not average costs. Practice protects against plausible execution of the wrong mission and conflicting dependencies. [CALL, pp. 49–54](https://api.army.mil/e2/c/downloads/2023/01/19/48e6a637/19-18-commander-and-staff-guide-to-rehearsals-a-no-fail-approach-handbook-jul-19-public.pdf)
Failure/carry-over: Army leaders report orders arriving late and overloaded with detail; their remedy stresses short orders and quick confirmation of understanding. Copy purpose, end state, constraints and subordinate initiative; rehearse only consequential interactions. Military discipline and extensive prior training do not transfer automatically. [Army professional account](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Journals/Military-Review/English-Edition-Archives/September-October-2019/Townsend-Mission-Command-III/)

### 3. Surgery and anaesthesia
Procedure: a designated coordinator leads checks before induction, before incision and before departure; the incision pause includes identity/site/procedure and each profession's anticipated hazards. The surgeon is required at the latter two checks; the first requires at least nurse and anaesthetist. Named instructions use sender → receiver's read-back → sender's confirmation. [WHO manual](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/patient-safety/9789241598590-eng.pdf), [AHRQ check-back](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/checkback.html)
Attention/protection: three team gates, not three new approvals from an absent executive; WHO describes the incision pause as under a minute, but total added attention is unmeasured here. An eight-hospital before/after study found complications fell from 11% to 7% and deaths from 1.5% to 0.8%; this does not isolate the checklist from implementation. [WHO guideline introduction](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK143229/), [WHO evidence summary](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/safe-surgery-saves-lives-frequently-asked-questions)
Failure/carry-over: mandatory adoption in Ontario did not significantly improve operative outcomes; WHO recommends leadership, training, coaching, feedback and local adaptation. Copy a short, spoken gate immediately before an irreversible act and explicit confirmation; clinical hazard lists and authority structures are domain-specific. [Ontario study](https://www.ices.on.ca/publications/journal-articles/introduction-of-surgical-safety-checklists-in-ontario-canada/), [WHO implementation guidance](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241598590)

### 4. Hospital wards
Procedure: outgoing clinician hands over severity, patient summary, action list with owner/time, contingencies, then receiver synthesis: I-PASS. Ward boards expose patient plans and discharge obstacles; board rounds allocate work. NEWS2 couples observed state to named response competence and observation frequency. [I-PASS form](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/ipass.html), [board-round study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37793675/), [NEWS2, chart 4](https://www.rcp.ac.uk/media/a4ibkkbf/news2-final-report_0_0.pdf)
Attention/protection: an I-PASS bundle study associated 23% fewer errors and 30% fewer preventable adverse events with oral handoff time essentially unchanged: 2.4 versus 2.5 minutes per patient. This measured clinician handoff, not executive attention or the mnemonic alone. [Primary study](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMsa1405556)
Failure/carry-over: a stale printed handoff survived correct training; the reported remedy added senior review/presence for new interns. Alarm exposure was associated with slower responses; remedies include patient-specific thresholds and better electrode care. Copy timestamped state, if/then contingencies and a responder ladder; do not copy physiological thresholds or assume a board reduces meetings by itself. [Handoff case](https://psnet.ahrq.gov/web-mm/written-signout-it-only-works-if-you-use-right-one), [alarm study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25873486/), [professional remedies](https://psnet.ahrq.gov/perspective/reducing-safety-hazards-monitor-alert-and-alarm-fatigue)

### 5. Aviation
Procedure: prioritize warnings, cautions and advisories; suppress unnecessary alerts rather than treating every state as urgent. During taxi, takeoff, landing and flight below 10,000 feet except cruise, restrict nonessential cockpit activity. Controllers keep current flight strips, update clearances without erasing history, and remove strips when no longer needed. [FAA alerting, §§7–12](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_25.1322-1.pdf), [sterile flight deck, §8.5](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_120-48A.pdf), [strip rules](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/atc_html/chap2_section_3.html)
Attention/protection: pilots/controllers still monitor continuously; a quiet alert panel is not a system unattended by humans. New student pilots earn bounded solo permission through instructor assessment, with renewed endorsements; no measured minutes saved were found. [FAA student training](https://www.faa.gov/pilots/become/student_training)
Failure/carry-over: FAA warns false alerts destroy confidence and misunderstanding sterile-cockpit rules can silence urgent cabin warnings; train the emergency exception. Copy prioritized exceptions, an explicit monitor, a current work strip and scoped permissions. **Untraced:** a universal “dark cockpit means everything is well” rule; the sourced alerting procedure is narrower. [FAA alerting](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_25.1322-1.pdf), [FAA communication](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_120-48A.pdf)

### 6. Construction and architecture
Procedure: agree brief/budget/programme, show concept sketches/models, then produce coordinated drawings and specifications. AIA B109 distinguishes owner/architect approval of visual mock-ups from testing performance mock-ups. At handover, practical completion comes with a defects list rather than pretending every detail is finished. [RSUA stages](https://rsua.org.uk/what-is-working-with-an-architect-like/), [AIA B109 §§4.3–4.4](https://assets.aiacontracts.com/ctrzdweb02/zdpdfs/preview_b109-2020.pdf)
Attention/protection: owner reviews phase outputs and appearance-critical mock-ups; architect handles inspections and queries. Number of meetings and durations are contract-specific and unmeasured. These are professional/contractual gates, not a controlled estimate of rework saved. [RSUA](https://rsua.org.uk/what-is-working-with-an-architect-like/), [AIA agreement](https://assets.aiacontracts.com/ctrzdweb02/zdpdfs/preview_b109-2020.pdf)
Failure/carry-over: the contract separates design approval, technical performance and remaining defects; treating a visual approval as proof of performance crosses those boundaries. Proposal: show a cheap representation for taste, test a working slice for behavior, then walk the deliverable against an owned defects list. Construction's physical irreversibility makes its full phase apparatus excessive for many small software tasks. [AIA §§4.3.4, 4.4.1](https://assets.aiacontracts.com/ctrzdweb02/zdpdfs/preview_b109-2020.pdf)

### 7. Restaurant kitchens
Procedure: expo calls a ticket including special instructions; stations call back; the ticket stays on the rail. Expo fires courses, negotiates a pickup time, writes that time on the ticket, checks plates at the pass and asks runners for hands. The chef/practitioner example explicitly permits a station to say it needs more time. [Practitioner's procedure](https://saltandlove.blog/2024/03/08/expo-and-the-pass/), [Escoffier terminology](https://www.escoffier.edu/blog/culinary-arts/more-useful-examples-of-chef-jargon/)
Attention/protection: expo attends essentially every ticket, course and release; diner/owner does not coordinate cooks. Timing is continuous and highly perishable; no general seconds-per-order or comparative efficacy study was traced. This is professional first-person practice evidence, not a universal kitchen standard. [Expo account](https://saltandlove.blog/2024/03/08/expo-and-the-pass/)
Failure/carry-over: partial tables leave food waiting under lamps and lose quality; expo coordinates station capacity and complete pickups. Copy one release point, dependency-aware batching and permission to reject an impossible ETA. Do not infer a universal numeric WIP cap or forbid useful speculative software work just because food spoils. [Expo account](https://saltandlove.blog/2024/03/08/expo-and-the-pass/)

### 8. Newsrooms and publishing
Procedure: a shared news budget shows assignments, developing stories and ready copy; editors use it to allocate coverage. The Post describes assignment-editor review, copy-editor review and senior oversight. Risk-sensitive work has designated senior review; Reuters publishes explicit correction rules. [Editor interview / working budget](https://www.storybench.org/time-saving-story-assignment-tool-gives-new-hampshire-newspaper-edge/), [Post policy](https://www.washingtonpost.com/discussions/2021/01/01/policies-and-standards/), [Reuters standards](https://reutersagency.com/about/standards-values/)
Attention/protection: editor commissions and judges publication; desk staff check copy. Meetings and final reads recur per edition or publication cycle; no universal frequency or measured duration was traced. The interviewed editor reports a shared form/sheet replaced walking around to discover assignments; that is testimony, not a time study. [Budget account](https://www.storybench.org/time-saving-story-assignment-tool-gives-new-hampshire-newspaper-edge/)
Failure/carry-over: one tip produced five independent reporting calls; visible ownership addressed duplication. Corrections must remain explicit rather than silently rewriting the past. Copy a shared assignment record, separate editorial judgment from mechanical checks, and preserve corrections. Exact editor markup is house-specific; no universal mark-up template was verified. [Budget account](https://www.storybench.org/time-saving-story-assignment-tool-gives-new-hampshire-newspaper-edge/), [AP correction procedure](https://www.ap.org/about/news-values-and-principles/telling-the-story/)

### 9. Film and theatre production
Procedure: production distributes the next day's call sheets/schedules at wrap; ADs route cast, surface delays, and protect the director's focus. Call sheets distinguish unit call, departmental pre-calls and locations. Footage goes to post with script, camera, sound and technical notes. Theatre's prompt book binds numbered departmental cues to script/action; operator acknowledges standby before an explicit go. [ScreenSkills daily procedure](https://www.screenskills.com/media/hmofxa0s/typical-working-day-during-the-shoot-word-format.pdf), [first AD](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/film-and-tv-drama/production-management/assistant-director/), [cue protocol](https://theatrecrafts.com/pages/home/topics/stage-management/calls-and-cans/)
Attention/protection: director still judges performances/shots; AD coordinates continuously. Showcaller executes every cue while the director need not reapprove a rehearsed show. Daily paperwork/footage and per-cue checks are documented; director review minutes and comparative efficacy are unmeasured. [ScreenSkills](https://www.screenskills.com/media/hmofxa0s/typical-working-day-during-the-shoot-word-format.pdf), [prompt book](https://theatrecrafts.com/pages/home/topics/stage-management/the-prompt-book/)
Failure/carry-over: stale script/call-sheet versions cause missed requirements; revisions are approved and distinctly identified before distribution. Ambiguous cue verbs are prohibited: only go initiates a cue. Copy versioned execution instructions, artifact-plus-notes and prepare/acknowledge/execute states; a novel software goal cannot be fully scripted like a rehearsed show. [ScreenSkills](https://www.screenskills.com/media/hmofxa0s/typical-working-day-during-the-shoot-word-format.pdf), [cue protocol](https://theatrecrafts.com/pages/home/topics/stage-management/calls-and-cans/)

### 10. Law and professional services
Procedure: junior records the assignment, says it back, clarifies time/budget/deliverable, and raises questions. Supervisor agrees review stages and lists drafts requiring approval, including novel advice and key documents. The Official Solicitor publishes standing instructions specifying the instructing authority and close supervision of delegated work; professional libraries supply maintained precedents. [Illinois law library](https://libguides.law.illinois.edu/c.php?g=384503&p=4615961), [SRA guidance](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/effective-supervision-guidance/), [standing instructions §2](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6808bb3d148a9969d2394edc/standard-instructionsFinalApril2025.pdf), [precedent service](https://www.bristollawsociety.com/news/the-law-society-law-library/)
Attention/protection: experienced staff doing standard low-risk work may be sampled; trainees need more checking, and complex matters may require intermediate review. The partner/supervisor remains accountable; the client's own involvement is not the same as partner effort. There is no universal sample percentage, review duration or causal outcome estimate in this guidance. [SRA](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/effective-supervision-guidance/)
Failure/carry-over: SRA rejects sparse sampling of high-volume, high-risk work and requires samples beyond what juniors volunteer. Copy task-specific delegation, review triggered by risk, explicit reserved decisions and independent sample selection. Precedents do not establish that a preference or permission applies to every new fact pattern; regulated duties cannot be delegated away. [SRA](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/effective-supervision-guidance/)

### 11. Manufacturing
Procedure: abnormality triggers a call button/andon and local help; work stops until resolved. Downstream processes replenish what they have consumed using kanban. Acceptance inspection has normal, tightened and reduced regimes with explicit switching conditions, rather than steadily increasing freedom with every pass. [Toyota plant procedure](https://global.toyota/en/company/plant-tours/production-system/), [ASQ regimes](https://asq.org/quality-resources/z14-z19)
Attention/protection: supervisor handles exceptions; machines and operators detect them. Inspection staff apply sampling plans; principal minutes are unmeasured. Toyota documents operation, while historical FAO guidance specifies operating rules and sampling risk; neither proves a particular software trust score. [Toyota](https://global.toyota/en/company/plant-tours/production-system/), [FAO historical guidance §§2.2.16, 4.2.2.4](https://www.fao.org/4/j2291e/j2291e04.htm)
Failure/carry-over: reduced inspection requires a continuing comparable stream, stable production, sufficient clean history and authority agreement; rejection or instability restores normal inspection. Copy a real stop condition, downstream capacity and reversible inspection regimes. Correlated software failures and changed models/tasks break the stable-lot assumption; passing jobs are not interchangeable specimens. [FAO historical scheme](https://www.fao.org/4/j2291e/j2291e04.htm)

### 12. Emergency dispatch / incident command
Procedure: ICS assigns responsibility and objectives; ICS 201 records a map/situation, hazards, objectives/actions, organization and resource status for transfer to an incoming commander. Radio operator requests a particular recipient read back specified material; sender corrects errors. Receipt, understanding and commitment have distinct phraseology. [FEMA ICS 201](https://training.fema.gov/emiweb/is/icsresource/assets/ics%20forms/ics%20form%20201%2C%20incident%20briefing%20%28v3%29.pdf), [Rhode Island radio procedure](https://dem.ri.gov/emergency-response-plan/section-4/policies-standard-procedures-emergency-responders), [NSW fire phraseology](https://www.rfs.nsw.gov.au/resources/publications/doctrine/reference/radio-communications-phraseology)
Attention/protection: commander briefs at start/transfer and directs operations as needed; dispatchers and section chiefs coordinate continuously. FEMA's preferred 1:5 span is explicitly a guideline. No measured executive minutes saved were found; the form externalizes state and reduces ambiguity about ownership. [FEMA training](https://emilms.fema.gov/_is0700b/groups/203.html), [ICS 201](https://training.fema.gov/emiweb/is/icsresource/assets/ics%20forms/ics%20form%20201%2C%20incident%20briefing%20%28v3%29.pdf)
Failure/carry-over: undelivered relays must be reported; wrong read-backs are corrected, not treated as receipts. Copy named ownership, explicit takeover and durable status separate from messages. Unified Command exists: this is not proof that every situation needs one all-powerful human. Emergency response timings do not prescribe software deadlines. [Rhode Island procedure](https://dem.ri.gov/emergency-response-plan/section-4/policies-standard-procedures-emergency-responders), [ICS 201 organization instructions](https://training.fema.gov/emiweb/is/icsresource/assets/ics%20forms/ics%20form%20201%2C%20incident%20briefing%20%28v3%29.pdf)

## Ten procedures most worth copying — proposed design rules

1. **Prepare the decision before requesting attention:** issue, recommended action, real alternatives, deadline/reason and cleared evidence; background opens only if needed. [DWP form](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7f4e1be5274a2e87db50bc/foi-248-2011-ministerial-sub.pdf)
2. **Confirm understanding before examining a plan:** ask what outcome, purpose and constraints the helper understood; request a later backbrief only when execution risk warrants it. [Army distinction](https://api.army.mil/e2/c/downloads/2023/01/19/48e6a637/19-18-commander-and-staff-guide-to-rehearsals-a-no-fail-approach-handbook-jul-19-public.pdf)
3. **Pause at the irreversible boundary:** name a coordinator and obtain active checks from the relevant roles, rather than another generic approval screen. [WHO](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/patient-safety/9789241598590-eng.pdf)
4. **Handover actions, owners, times and contingencies; require receiver synthesis.** Keep the approved version visible during the handover. [I-PASS](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/ipass.html), [stale-copy failure](https://psnet.ahrq.gov/web-mm/written-signout-it-only-works-if-you-use-right-one)
5. **Make quietness depend on active detection:** name who watches, what triggers an exception and who responds; reserve an urgent interruption path. [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_120-48A.pdf), [Toyota](https://global.toyota/en/company/plant-tours/production-system/)
6. **Separate appearance approval from performance evidence:** use a sketch/mock-up for judgment and a tested slice for behavior, with remaining defects explicitly owned. [AIA](https://assets.aiacontracts.com/ctrzdweb02/zdpdfs/preview_b109-2020.pdf), [RSUA](https://rsua.org.uk/what-is-working-with-an-architect-like/)
7. **Release a complete useful batch through one pass:** negotiate dependency readiness and reviewer capacity before queuing a showing. [Kitchen procedure](https://saltandlove.blog/2024/03/08/expo-and-the-pass/), [Toyota pull](https://global.toyota/en/company/plant-tours/production-system/)
8. **Give each live assignment one current record:** owner, next action and release state; corrections supersede visibly rather than erasing history. [News budget](https://www.storybench.org/time-saving-story-assignment-tool-gives-new-hampshire-newspaper-edge/), [FAA strips](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/atc_html/chap2_section_3.html)
9. **Distinguish prepared, acknowledged and executed:** a receipt or readiness acknowledgment must never count as completion or permission to execute. [Theatre cues](https://theatrecrafts.com/pages/home/topics/stage-management/calls-and-cans/), [radio receipt rules](https://dem.ri.gov/emergency-response-plan/section-4/policies-standard-procedures-emergency-responders)
10. **Earn less checking for a defined work class, and reverse it when conditions change:** choose samples independently; retain explicit exclusions for high-risk work. [SRA](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/effective-supervision-guidance/), [historical sampling scheme](https://www.fao.org/4/j2291e/j2291e04.htm)

## Wordings worth copying

- Decision headings: “Issue”, “Recommendation”, “Timing”; decision verbs: “Agree that”, “Note that”. Put one requested action on each line. [DWP](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7f4e1be5274a2e87db50bc/foi-248-2011-ministerial-sub.pdf)
- Risk question: “What are the critical or non-routine steps?” [WHO](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/patient-safety/9789241598590-eng.pdf)
- Close the loop with “Correct.” after a substantive read-back; “got it” alone leaves the sender's verification out. [AHRQ](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/checkback.html)
- Ready/execution: “Standby LX Q 11” → “Standing By” → “LX Q 11 Go”. [Theatrecrafts](https://theatrecrafts.com/pages/home/topics/stage-management/calls-and-cans/)
- Negotiated ETA: “I need seven!” followed by an agreed pickup time written on the ticket. [Kitchen practitioner](https://saltandlove.blog/2024/03/08/expo-and-the-pass/)
- Precise repair: “READ BACK TIME”; “SAY AGAIN”; “WRONG”, followed by the correct version. Distinguish receipt from commitment; NSW defines “Wilco” as “Message received, understood and I will comply.” [RI](https://dem.ri.gov/emergency-response-plan/section-4/policies-standard-procedures-emergency-responders), [NSW](https://www.rfs.nsw.gov.au/resources/publications/doctrine/reference/radio-communications-phraseology)
- **Adaptations, not quotations:** “I will deliver [artifact] by [time]; done means [checks]; I may decide [scope]; stop and ask if [condition].” “If [trigger], do [action]; if that fails, contact [owner] by [time].” These combine intent, handoff and escalation procedures above; their software effectiveness is untested.

## Numbers worth knowing — context, not universal interface limits

- **Briefing size/time:** DWP: issue ≤100 words; decision front page 1 page; discussion ≤2 A4 pages; target reading <5 minutes; routine response usually 5 working days. This limits document size, not the number of unrelated decisions. [DWP](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7f4e1be5274a2e87db50bc/foi-248-2011-ministerial-sub.pdf)
- **Order confirmation:** CALL's chapter-5 technique: session ≤15 minutes; subsequent backbrief ≤10 minutes per subordinate. The handbook contains other echelon-specific limits; these are not universal averages. [CALL](https://api.army.mil/e2/c/downloads/2023/01/19/48e6a637/19-18-commander-and-staff-guide-to-rehearsals-a-no-fail-approach-handbook-jul-19-public.pdf)
- **Span:** FEMA favors 1 supervisor:5 subordinates, adjusting to circumstances; older training gives 3–7. Neither supports “seven pending decisions” as a cognitive law. [Current course](https://emilms.fema.gov/_is0700b/groups/203.html), [older training](https://emilms.fema.gov/_is_0200c/groups/242.html)
- **Alert burden:** HSE's process-industry guide: steady-state <1 alarm/10 minutes likely acceptable; 1/5 minutes manageable; 1/2 minutes likely excessive demand; >1/minute likely unacceptable. A hospital study found 87.1% of PICU and 99.0% of ward alarms nonactionable. These are different domains, not software notification quotas. [HSE, pp. 35–36](https://www.hse.gov.uk/offshore/assets/docs/inspection-of-loss-of-containment.pdf), [hospital study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25873486/)
- **Escalation:** NEWS2: score 5–6 or one parameter at 3 requires at least hourly observations; ≥7 requires continuous monitoring, with response competence specified. Copy the explicit trigger/response coupling, not these clinical values. [RCP chart 4](https://www.rcp.ac.uk/media/a4ibkkbf/news2-final-report_0_0.pdf)
- **Trust and sampling:** historical FAO/ISO-based scheme tightens after 2 rejected lots in 5 or fewer; reduced inspection needs ≥10 accepted lots plus a defect-count limit, stable production and authority agreement; typical reduced samples are 2/5 of normal. Restore normal after rejection/instability. This is a historical worked procedure, not current ISO compliance advice or a suitable rate for heterogeneous software tasks. [FAO §§2.2.16, 4.2.2.4](https://www.fao.org/4/j2291e/j2291e04.htm)
- **Bounded permission:** FAA student solo endorsement renews every 90 days; it is scope-specific assessed permission, not permanent general trust. [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/pilots/become/student_training)

## Warnings and remaining gaps

- **Less principal attention can require more staff attention.** Expo, AD, controller and showcaller are busy jobs; two principal contacts do not imply two total coordination contacts. This is the cross-field design inference from §§5, 7 and 9, not a measured saving.
- **A green report can be stale; a receipt can be merely transport.** Version the evidence and verify the receiving role actually took responsibility. [Handoff failure](https://psnet.ahrq.gov/web-mm/written-signout-it-only-works-if-you-use-right-one), [RI distinctions](https://dem.ri.gov/emergency-response-plan/section-4/policies-standard-procedures-emergency-responders)
- **A copied form is not an implemented practice.** Check actual participation and outcomes, not completion ticks alone; the Ontario counterexample prevents claiming checklist adoption guarantees success. [Ontario study](https://www.ices.on.ca/publications/journal-articles/introduction-of-surgical-safety-checklists-in-ontario-canada/)
- **Quiet must retain a route for bad news; samples must include inconvenient work.** Neither “do not interrupt” nor high volume excuses disabling escalation or scrutiny. [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_120-48A.pdf), [SRA](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/effective-supervision-guidance/)
