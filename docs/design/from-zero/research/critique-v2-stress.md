## Comparison

This is the central correction revision 3 made after the replay.
Letter does better at named recording steps, marking a wait, requiring viewed widths, changing local permissions and telling a checked repair without another acceptance. Its explicit loss account is more honest than a reassuring return headline. Merge those mechanics into Sitting; keep recording, trying the stand-in and using the actual device explicitly separate.
The difference is not merely feel: early versus late decisions, reachable versus undrawn later work, stateful revocation versus an acknowledgement, and custody of recorded and tried versions change what he must do and what can go wrong. Their visual grammar has converged; their causal promises have not.
