# Final critique: count, defects and honesty

Put **v2-letter** forward as the main design, after the safety and honesty fixes below.
It carries fewer ceremonial acceptances, and its choices and undo actually change more of the visible state.
The sitting is the better early showing and shorter read; its permission, stop and correction receipts are less trustworthy.
Neither is ready to be presented as a working demonstration: all sixteen supplied self-check runs passed despite the failures below.
The difference is substantive: when work starts, when choices arrive, what silence permits, and whether an answer survives.
