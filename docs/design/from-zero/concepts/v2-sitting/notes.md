# The sitting, round 2

**What I built.** One doorway: a card on his lock screen, a widget he chooses to add. It says what has gathered and how long it will take, how fresh that is, or that it cannot see; it has a mic. Tapping it opens one sheet, written like a letter. Each thing opens in turn and folds to one line in his blue ink, with Undo. The sheet ends with "That is all." On the desk the sheet is the left page and the thing itself the facing page. Below, never pushed, are three pages: where all 40 goals stand, what he has let me do, and who is working where.

`index.html` carries the 14 events and the stress cases in 15 moments; the striped bar is not part of the design. `#check` (add `&t=light` or `&t=dark`) walks every moment through the real buttons and runs the side paths. It fails on:
- a word of the method on screen;
- a control under 44 px;
- a sideways scroll;
- "nothing needs you" shown while anything waits or is unseen.

It passes at 390 and 1280, light and dark.

## Three merges in this round

**The say-back that does not wait** (revision 3):
- It arrives as one told line when work starts; any line can be fixed at any time.
- Only Sales tax waits for his go, and says why.
- His decisions sit inside the first showing, as two concrete alternatives.
- No "agree" items remain.

**Told, not asked:** only when nothing is left to judge, the result can be undone, and it is inside what he allowed. One told result comes back at random as a real look.

**The showing sheet** (v2-showing):
- A seal naming exactly what was checked, and "Looks right · build 4.12" naming it again.
- Done lines as dots that jump to their moment.
- "Your yes does not cover…".
- Marks that come back green in place.
- Before what cannot be undone: said back, a choice of who gets it, press and hold. That answer is never pinned; it sits after the sentence it answers.

**The return** (v2-return):
- **After five days:** "36 of your 40 goals need nothing from you" over one bar that adds up to 40. What changed is sorted by consequence. What needs him is cut to his minutes, and the rest is named, dated and costed ("Answer by Wednesday noon, or it misses Thursday's release").
- **A wrong result:** what it touched comes sorted (built on it; not used yet; checked again and fine; not looked at). The remedy is one prepared choice, with the exact note to his accountant said back.
- **A wrong rule:** the checker's own words; its uses sorted (inside its case, fine; already charged; held).
- **What you've let me do:** a calm page where each permission shows where it came from, its uses and when it lapses. Nothing on it asks for upkeep.
- **Blind and away:** whole-blind (the phone works it out itself) and half-blind (only the unseen lines hatched).
- **The one buzz:** tried at most twice, then it stops and says what was lost.
- **Stop:** for one goal, one project, or everything. Each says what it did and what was already out of hand.

## From round 1's concepts

- **Sitting:** "Yours to judge" first; the split when more has gathered than he has time for; "Not now" with a latest date; the five verbs on each goal.
- **Letter:** the self-ageing glance; hold to say; ink marks; the buzz copy.
- **Picture:** goals in fixed places; struck-through dates; hatching; the facing page.
- **Thread:** marking by touch; Undo on every receipt.

## The three unsolved problems

**1. Things that move. In part.**
- Each showing is sealed to its exact build, page or file.
- A journey is a recording he scrubs; a dot jumps to its moment, and a touch pins one.
- "Try it on your phone" opens the same build, and marks come back.
- Web pages at two widths, with "Use it" kept apart from "Mark".
- Jobs as what they did; speed as ten runs each.
- **Not solved:** feel and sound are judged on frames.

**2. Coming back. Answered, with one honest limit.**
- One bar that adds up, then consequence order, his minutes, and the rest costed.
- A miss and a wrong rule arrive with what they touched, already sorted.
- **Limit:** each return is bounded, not the total. Debt is named and stops growing; it does not shrink.

**3. Rhythm. Answered.**
- Things gather for when he usually looks.
- A default goes only after a look in which he could have seen it.
- "Do them now" pulls things early.
- Absence needs no word from him.

## Three weakest points

1. **Moving things are still recordings.**
2. **The return cost words.** The sorted trace and the said-back note added about 155 words to the events.
3. **A say-back he never opens can drift** until the first showing.

## What revision 3 still cannot be drawn

- **The checker's direct line** (principle 8): its answer still waits for his next look.
- **What was built on what** (principle 11) needs the record to hold the links between results.
- **A known gap he accepts** ("your yes does not cover") has no name in the method.
- **The random real look** needs a rate per kind of work.
- **Undo is not free.** The method treats undo as costless, but undoing a default that others now rest on is the same trace problem as a wrong result.
