# The sitting, round 2

**What I built.** One doorway: his lock screen. It says what has gathered and how long it will take, how fresh that is, or that it cannot see. It has a mic for saying what he wants. Tapping it opens one sheet: a short sitting, written like a letter. Each thing in it opens in turn, and each answer folds to one line in his blue ink, with Undo. The sheet ends with "That is all." On the desk the sheet is the left page and the thing itself sits on the facing page.

Below the sheet, never pushed, are three pages: where all 40 goals stand, what he has let me do, and who is working where. Each goal has a page with five verbs: pause, change what we agreed, take back what I may decide, undo the last change, stop.

`index.html` carries the 14 events and every stress case in 15 moments. The striped bar is not part of the design. `#check` walks every moment through the real buttons at either width and reports on screen. Both widths pass.

## What I took from each concept

- **Sitting (the base).** The bounded sitting and its running order. Every item opens with "Only you can judge…". Judgement is kept apart from proof: the proof is one line, "✓ 6 checks, not by the maker", plus "Not tried: …" only when something wasn't tried. Also kept: the five verbs per goal, the honest split on coming back, and "Not now" with a latest date. Removed: Begin, the segmented progress bar, ✕ and minute counters. The sheet no longer looks like a lesson.
- **Letter.** The lock-screen glance, which counts its own age. Hold to say what you want, with "That is not what I said" and "Say it back now". Strike-through, notes and pins in his ink on the thing itself. The P.S. trust offer straight after the third acceptance. The buzz copy: "Not in this morning's sitting because the store ended your sign-in at noon. First buzz in three weeks." The paper-and-ink look.
- **Picture.** Forty goals in fixed places under four projects. Each project shows only what changed and folds the rest ("and 12 more, on their dates"). A moved date is struck through on the goal's own line. Hatched "unseen" lines and the heading "I can't vouch for 2 lines". The two-page desk. "You said so" kept apart from "my guesses".
- **Thread.** Marking on the result itself, with the note at the mark. Undo on every receipt. A "For example" scene under the done-lines. Plain computer names one level down ("the one on the shelf").

## The three unsolved problems

**1. Things that move. Answered in part.**
- Every showing carries the exact thing it was made from: "Build 4.12 · recorded on a real phone", "As served at 07:55", "made 15:58". Marks attach to that thing.
- **Phone journey** (Faster sign-up): a recording of the checked build that he can play and scrub step by step. Mark pins a spot on a screen, stamped with the step's time ("0:14 · screen 2"). "Try it on your phone" opens the same build with a pencil on top, and his mark comes back onto the recording.
- **Web page:** shown at phone and desk width, side by side on the desk and switched on the phone. "Use it" and "Mark" are kept apart, so pointing never presses Buy.
- **Background job** (Move photos): what it did, with times, a sample, and the one part that can't be undone, dated.
- **Speed:** ten timed runs before and after, same checks, same computer.
- **Release:** shown as the exact act: what, to whom (about 3,400 students), the pinned build, and that there is no undo.
- **Marks come back:** the next sketch shows each of his marks and what changed (Sketch 2 / Sketch 3).
- **Not solved:** feel, timing, sound and car or map journeys are judged on frames, not the live thing. The phone round trip is drawn, not built.

**2. Coming back. Answered for one level.**
- **After five days:** three lines first: nothing went wrong (rechecked at 8:00), done without you (undoable until Monday), decided for you. Then only his usual minutes; the rest gathers for 5 pm, and "Do them now" pulls it in early.
- **A bad result with work built on it:** comes first, as "My miss": what was wrong, what used it (paused before sending, rebuilt, not sent), what changes, and the fixed file.
- **A wrong rule (day 30):** shows where the rule came from and that it was my guess, what it decided since, that I've already stopped using it, and one choice. The money it cost is repaid as an exact act.
- **A week of skipped sittings:** "I've slowed down: 6 new things are held, not started," and "Carry on" restarts them.
- **He never curates:** I propose the repair and he picks one answer.
- **Not solved:** chains deeper than one result built on another. And "What you've let me do" is still a list he could feel obliged to keep tidy.

**3. Rhythm. Answered.**
- Things gather for the times he usually looks, and the lock card says what gathered ("3 things · about 7 minutes · gathered for your 8:30"). The habit he needs is looking at his lock screen, which he already does.
- A default that can go ahead does so only after his next sitting ("after your 5 pm sitting"), so he always gets one sitting to see it. One that can't wait says so and never fires.
- **Pulling early:** "Look now" on the lock card, "Do them now" on the split, and "Next sketch as soon as it's ready", which keeps a taste loop to minutes and arrives without a sound.
- **The one interruption:** has its three-part test, says why it wasn't in a sitting, counts itself as my miss, and turns into a rule. Away is one sentence, or the same slow-down when he skips.

## Three weakest points

1. **Moving things are still shown as recordings.** A recording, frames and a drawn hand-off stand in for the running build. Corrections about timing or feel become a pin on a frame plus words.
2. **Recovery is one level deep.** The miss traces one dependent goal and the wrong rule decided three things. A real day 30, with several rules and results entangled, would need a trace view I have not drawn.
3. **Holding work back is narrated, not shown.** "6 new things are held" is a sentence. He cannot see how they were chosen or what holding them costs. And reading is still about 2,500 words for the core week.

## What revision 2 still cannot be drawn

- **The checker's direct line.** Principle 8 promises he can ask the checker around me. The answer needs a place to land. I put it in his next sitting, so the checker's voice waits for his rhythm.
- **End dates on rules.** Principle 4 asks every wider rule for one, but nothing in the record says when a rule should expire, so I could not draw one honestly.
- **What was built on what.** Principle 11 ("what was built on it is traced") needs the record to hold which results used which. Section 4 lists goals, rulings and results, but not that link.
- **A wrong forecast of the next sitting.** Principle 12 defines defaults against his next sitting, but the next sitting is a forecast. The method should say what happens when it is wrong. My rule: nothing goes ahead before he has had one sitting to see it.
- **The glance.** It carried more of every problem than any other moment. The method still lists it as one of five boxes, without its freshness, partial-blind and held-work states.
