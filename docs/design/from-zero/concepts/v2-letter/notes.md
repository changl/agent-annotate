# The sheet: one design from the best of four

**What I built.** One sheet. On his phone it sits behind a glance he put on the lock screen; on his desk it is the left page of an open book, with the thing itself on the facing page. He sits at two times he set (8:30 and 5:00), but it opens at any time. A sitting holds only what is his: look, decide, and the rare "go?" before something that cannot be undone. Everything else is told in one line with See and Undo, or is silent. It ends with "That is all."

Open `index.html`. "next" in the striped bar steps through 15 moments of the stress week; "notes" says what he does not see. Under 1040 px it is the phone; above, the desk. `#check` runs every state and reports on screen. `#k=back&th=signup` and the like open one state.

## Changed for revision 3 of the method and the count critique

- **No agreement waits for him.** What I heard arrives as the work starts: one told line ("I have started. First look: a sketch on Thursday") with "What I heard" one touch away. Any line can be touched and changed at any time. The nine sittings now hold 13 things, not 19. One say-back still waits for his go (sales tax), and says why.
- **His decisions sit on the showing.** The three sign-up questions no longer come as a list on the bad day. They wait on the screens they are about: a dot marks the screen, and choosing changes the screen in front of him. The ratings choice sits under the sketch and changes what the instructor sees.
- **Told, not asked.** The corrected invoice file and the yearly price are no longer put to him. The miss is told first, with what was built on it, "See the corrected file" and "Hold it". Yearly first is told with "See both", where he can still pick the other or say "Neither…".
- **The glance is his own widget.** He put it there; it makes no sound, never lights the screen, and keeps one shape whether or not a sitting is due. "Take it off" is one touch, and leaves a plain "Open your sheet". By the critics' rule an opt-in preview is not a delivery.

## The three unsolved problems

**1. A thing that moves.** Answered in part.
- *A journey*: the three screens run in the sheet and he taps through them. "Use it" and "Mark it" are kept apart. He marks a spot, or the wait between two screens; each mark keeps its screen. It cannot be accepted until he has walked it and settled what is his.
- *A page at two widths*: one page at phone width and at 1280, its own buttons working; marks keep their width; both must be seen. On the desk it can take the whole spread.
- *A job with nothing to look at*: told, with what ran line by line (each line markable), what was not tried, and Undo until the day that cannot be undone, which pauses for him.
- *A number*: the figure, what it was, the spread of ten runs in words, Undo.
- *What cannot be undone*: the exact thing, to whom, said back in one sentence, a held press. A later sign-in is only a sign-in.
- **Not answered:** a route, a drive, sound, a real payment; they are listed as "Not tried". "Running here as on a phone like yours" is a claim the prototype cannot prove.

**2. Coming back.** Answered for the cases drawn.
- *Five days away*: one sheet. "What the wait cost", then "Went ahead, inside what you allowed" (each with Undo), then "Held", then nine things split over three sittings, the costliest three first.
- *A week of two sittings*: no nagging; it slowed what starts and asks one question about pace.
- *A rule used too widely*: his words, its nine uses, the wrong one, what was done, one choice. He curates nothing.
- *A bad accepted result with work built on it*: told first, with each thing built on it and what was done.
- **Not answered:** the six things deferred on the return are named, not drawn. A trace deeper than one step has no drawn bound.

**3. Rhythm.** Answered.
- "From 5:00" means the next sitting time, whether or not he sits; then it is told once with Undo.
- He pulls early by opening the sheet ("Ready early"), "See what I heard" after speaking, or "Start it now" on a goal's page.
- The one interruption says why it could not wait, and the week counts it against me.

## Three weakest points

1. **The moving thing is still a stand-in.** What needs the real device, road or card is only listed as not tried, so a release can rest on proof he never saw.
2. **Told lines can be skimmed.** Ten things were told this week and none undone. That is cheap, and it is only safe while the checks can fail; the sheet cannot show him that they can.
3. **Work now starts on what I heard.** If I heard wrong and he does not open "What I heard", he finds out at the first look. The method accepts that cost; the sketch must really be cheap.

## What in revision 3 still cannot be drawn

- **Is a sitting a time or an act?** I chose the clock ("whether or not you sit"). The method does not say.
- **Which decisions wait for the showing.** I moved three questions onto the sign-up. That delayed them a week while the build went ahead on my picks. The method needs a rule for when a question is worth an early look of its own.
- **Admitting work at his rate (10).** I can draw "held". The method gives no order among forty goals.
- **A kind of work (9).** Nobody names the kinds or their edges. My day-30 miss is a "small wording change" that changed what people did.
- **The exact result (7).** I can print which build the checks ran on. He cannot see that it is true.
- **Cannot see, for a long time (5).** I invented: first line of the next sheet; reach him once if only he can fix it.
