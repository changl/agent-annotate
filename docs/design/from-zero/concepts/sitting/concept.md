# The sitting

**The idea.** Everything that needs him comes as one short, finite sitting that he opens when he chooses, and it ends with "That is all". Outside it there are only a dated promise that says how fresh it is, one way to say what he wants, and one kind of notification. (In `index.html` the striped bar is not part of the design: it steps through the week with `[` and `]` and switches light and dark.)

## Core components
| Component | What it is for | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| The door | The only screen outside a sitting. It says "3 things, about 8 minutes", or "Nothing needs you; next sitting about 5 pm", or, when the truth is stale, "I can't see the work right now", with the last known state. It never says "nothing" on stale information. | sight, trust |
| The running order | The whole sitting, before and during it: a verb (Agree, Look, Decide), the goal and the minutes. It is capped by minutes and by how long things have waited. | sight |
| The item | One goal at a time. It opens with "Only you can judge…", then shows the result as it really arrives, the checks by someone who did not make it, what was not tried, and what I assumed. | intent, sight, trust |
| Default lines | Each choice says what happens if he is silent, in one of two kinds. "Goes ahead" means it is inside authority he already gave and can be undone. "Waits" means it is parked or brought to him, because silence is not a yes. | trust |
| Pointing | He taps a line of the agreement or a part of the result, names the problem with a chip or by voice, and answers "here only, or always?" once. | intent |
| Not now | Parks the goal until an event, with a latest date, and it comes back by itself. | his memory |
| Told-once lines, the end | A moved date, a withdrawn question, or the watcher having been down is shown once. Then "That is all", the next sitting, and how fresh that is. | trust |
| Say it | The one input. It also stops, changes or takes back anything: "pause the price page". | intent |
| The interruption | The only notification: only he can act, it can't wait for his next sitting, and missing it costs a lot. Each one is reviewed and turned into a rule. The phone channel is tested and shows when. | trust |
| Silence line, everything moving | When the watcher last checked, and each goal's own freshness ("checked 2 min ago" or "unknown since 15:15"). The watcher has a backup on another computer. | trust |
| The goal page | Pause it, change what we agreed, take back what I may decide, undo its last change, stop it for good. It also holds the goal's record and cost. | trust |
| The original evidence | The checker's own report, unedited, one tap from every summary, and "ask the checker yourself, not through me". | sight, trust |
| The week's page | Discovery is kept apart from mistakes, bad news is listed, and trust is shown with what it rests on. It ends with "How did this week feel?". | trust |
| Who is working where | Two taps down: which helpers work where, and what each goal costs. | sight |

Behind the screen: the counterpart composes and sizes each sitting and keeps his rhythm; a watcher, plus a backup that watches the watcher; a checker who is never the maker; and the record. His own words are set in serif and mine in sans.

## The ten principles
1. Attention: each item opens with the one thing only he can judge. Sittings are sized in minutes, and the time he spends recovering is counted too.
2. Reacting: he says one sentence and reacts to what I heard, a sketch, and the result. Constraints and acceptance examples are written down as the "done means" lines.
3. Intent first: anything he says comes back as an agreement at his next sitting. It is reopened when new facts arrive, as Sales tax was when the accountant replied.
4. Decide once: "here only, or always?" becomes a rule that has a scope and can be taken back from the week's page.
5. Silence: quiet unless there is news, but always dated. "Unknown" is a visible state. The watcher has a backup. Kept promises are counted (21 of 22).
6. Nothing waits silently: every choice has an owner, a consequence and a time. A default acts only inside authority he already gave; otherwise it waits.
7. Show the thing: the result itself, checked line by line, with "not tried" listed and the checker's raw report one tap away.
8. One doorway: one app and one input. The original evidence and a direct line to the checker are always there.
9. Trust by kind of work: it rests on repeated outcomes, not acceptances. Random spot-checks continue, it resets when something changes back, and it is never handed over for what deletes, charges money or reaches customers.
10. Start no more: the limit is expected minutes and how long things have waited, not a count. While he is away, nothing new that needs him starts.

**Served worst: 10.** Holding work back is never visible to him, and Monday after the trip is still 16 minutes, 6 more than his usual 10.

## What the outside evidence changed
- Stale truth is shown as stale. There is a new moment, Wednesday 15:40, where the watcher itself goes quiet: the door says "I can't see the work", the list marks every goal "unknown since 15:15", and Thursday's door tells him once.
- Defaults are labelled "goes ahead" (yearly prices: layout was left to me, and it switches back) or "waits" (licence timing, parent emails, deleting photos, widening trust).
- Wording changes stop being shown only after 3 acceptances with none changed back. 1 in 5 is still shown at random, they reset if one is changed back, and never cover prices, terms or customers. A random spot-check of a trusted kind now appears on Wednesday.
- The cap is minutes and age. Monday offers "the first 3 now (7 min), the rest at 5 pm".
- Stop, change, take back and undo: an Undo after every answer, the goal page buttons, "Take back" on the week's page, and Say it for all of them.
- Original evidence: the checker's report is one tap away. Tuesday's report had listed month edges as "not tried", so Thursday's miss now traces back to a note I passed over. Untried cases now block a showing.
- Extra looks: the welcome email counts as discovery; the invoice export counts as the only waste. Bad news has its own place on the week's page.

## Count for the test week (phone, scripted path; words measured by `#count=1`)
- Places to look: **1**. Interruptions: **1**. Things to hold in his head: **0**, but sitting twice a day is a habit he must keep.
- Words read: **about 3,490**, up from 3,080 before these guards; the welcome email itself is 156 of them. The optional looks (the watcher being down, curiosity) add 530.
- Words typed **0**; spoken **20**. Taps **51**. Decisions **23**, of which **18** only he could make; the other 5 are acceptances and a spot-check while trust is still being built. Minutes: **about 45**.

## Left out, and what I would cut next
- **Left out:** any list of open questions, badges, a page per project, chat with helpers, search, a settings screen, putting two goals side by side.
- **Cut next:** the state words in the list of everything moving, the full check list on the phone, and "Stop for now" on the desk.

## Three weakest points
1. **Relations between goals depend on my grouping.** He cannot lay two items side by side.
2. **The rhythm is a hidden load.** Skipped sittings fire some defaults and stall the rest, and three days away became one 16-minute sitting. Being away moves the cost; it does not remove it.
3. **The guards cost words.** "Not tried" lists, default kinds, trust limits and raw reports added 13% more reading, and believing the silence still takes weeks of kept promises.

## Where I departed from the premise, and why
- **The door is not empty.** It shows how fresh the truth is, the interruption count, and one tap down to everything moving (principle 5).
- **One interruption exists** (event 8), kept rare by the three-part test and by reviewing each one.
- **The running order stays visible**, and spot-checks of trusted work enter sittings. One at a time serves attention, not hiding.
- **Comparison happens inside an item** (the two price pages), and related questions are grouped by goal.
- **Say it, the goal page and the original evidence exist outside sittings**, because stopping and getting around me must never wait for a sitting.
