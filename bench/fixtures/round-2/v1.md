# Rename orders.status

`orders.status` holds payment states from checkout and fulfillment states from
the warehouse in one text column. Every reader branches on who wrote the row;
INC-4471 came from a report that read `shipped` as paid. This plan splits the
column into `payment_state` and `fulfillment_state`.

## Readers and writers

| Service | Reads | Writes |
|---|---|---|
| checkout-api | yes | payment states |
| warehouse-worker | yes | fulfillment states |
| reporting | yes | no |
| support-console | yes | no |

## Plan

1. Add `payment_state` and `fulfillment_state`, both nullable text.
2. Dual-write: each writer sets `status` and its new column.
3. Backfill the 41M existing rows by splitting `status` values by writer.
4. Move readers to the new columns, one service per deploy.
5. Stop writing `status`, then drop it.

Every step ships as its own sqitch change with a revert. Nothing that locks
the table runs between 17:00 and 21:00 UTC (peak 1.8k orders/min).
