Resume wake-ups: parse Codex options, see Sends still in the outbox (PR #160 third review)

- Codex argv is parsed with the options `codex resume --help` lists:
  value options consume their value (`--profile resume` is not the
  subcommand); unknown options, -i, --last or a fork fail closed; binding
  needs positionals `resume ID`.
- The page server appends each automatic-delivery session_push under a new
  per-page outbox lock (a leaf lock, never held across an Orca call, so
  writes stay fast while dispatch is blocked). The wake-up checks the bus
  past the journal offset for that owner's Sends and writes its reservation
  under the same lock; a durable Send defers the re-arm.
