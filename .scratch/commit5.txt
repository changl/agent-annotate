Outbox locks get their own tree (PR #160 fourth review)

Outbox locks lived at deliveries/<project>/<slug>.outbox.lock, which is
the journal lock of a page named "<slug>.outbox" (dots are valid in
slugs). A watchdog re-binding both pages deadlocked on its own lock, and
plain Sends to one page waited behind the other's Orca call. They now live
at STATE_DIR/outbox-locks/<project>/<slug>.lock, which no journal reaches.
