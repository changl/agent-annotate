Resumed owners re-bind and get a turn after a restart (agent-annotate-dlj)

The 2.28.3 re-bind (eii) required the resumed session to be in the same
Orca terminal. Orca relaunches every resumed pane under a new handle, so
after the 2026-11-23 reboot no page re-bound and Sends queued until a
person typed into the terminal.

- Re-bind on same session + same Orca worktree once the old process is
  provably gone; the handle may change.
- The revive watchdog (60 s, at login) re-binds from outside: the one live
  claude/codex process resumed with the session id, its Orca terminal.
  Queued Sends then deliver and start a turn; an owner re-bound with
  nothing queued gets one wake-up to re-arm its wait loop (at most once).
- SessionStart waits up to 1 s for its payload instead of 10 ms.
- sweep.py owners: recently used pages whose owner is not running.
